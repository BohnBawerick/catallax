# Roadmap glossary

| Word | Meaning | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Phase | A block of work that ends in one joint release of Catallax and SolidDraft after a checkpoint. | Phase 5 |
| Step | A numbered goal within a phase, with a done-when line. Measure it at the end. Independent steps can run in parallel. | 5.1 Profile everything |
| Task | One worker, one branch, one pull request. The worker carrying a task is its lane. | Cache display meshes per face |

A **checkpoint** is the measured report and the owner's review at the end of a phase, before release. A **coverage line** is one of the 61 capabilities scored against OpenCASCADE and SMLib. Neither is a unit of work.

Use phase, step and task instead of "stage", "item N", "job" or "milestone" for roadmap work. These words still have their usual meanings in other contexts, such as a CI job or a construction stage. See the [shared roadmap](../ROADMAP.md) for steps and acceptance criteria.
