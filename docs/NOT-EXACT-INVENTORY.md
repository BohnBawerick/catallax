# `NotExact` producers, consumers and the P0 budget profile

This is the inventory that [ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md#migration-and-acceptance-order) requires before the composable error policy is declared enforced: every producer and consumer of an inexact decision, including passthrough and empty paths. It also records where the work goes in the three P0 cases that exceed the 1M scoreboard allowance. It is a source observation at `35fe6bf` plus the profiling feature added with it. It changes no kernel decision, work charge, allowance, tolerance or cap, and it does not claim that any part of the policy is enforced.

## 1. Where the work goes in the three P0 budget refusals

### Question and answer

PLAN section 3.1 says the expensive step is proving an exact zero and that any tolerance removes that step. If exact-zero proofs dominated the P0 budget refusals, the 1 nm decision rule (stage 2 job J4) would be the lever for them.

They do not. Exact-zero sign proofs take 12.4%, 0.4% and 2.1% of the complete work of block-and-sphere, crossing pipes and seam, and 28%, 2% and 13% of the first 1M units before each refusal. Removing every exact-zero proof and every enclosure that failed to separate zero would still leave the three requests at 2.35M, 9.10M and 6.30M units, between 2.4 and 9.1 times the 1M allowance. The dominant costs are elsewhere: high-precision coefficient enclosures in the imprint probe path, root isolation of an intersection's univariate polynomial, and cancellation checkpoints in the arctangent series during STEP export.

### Method

The `work-profile` feature of `catallax-numbers` attributes request work to the decider entry point that spent it, and splits `Decider::sign` by outcome and `Decider::enclose` by whether its ball separated zero. Attribution is exclusive of nested decider calls and keyed by the innermost logged operation and the calling source line. Observation reads the request counter; it never charges work or asks a numeric question. The `work_profile` example in `catallax-cli` answers one request through the same `verification::answer` path as `catallax-verify replay`.

```sh
cargo build --release -p catallax-cli --features work-profile --example work_profile
target/release/examples/work_profile parts/P0/seam.json --out "$PROOF/seam-1m" --max-work 1000000
target/release/examples/work_profile parts/P0/seam.json --out "$PROOF/seam-100m" --max-work 100000000
```

The output reports `summary.work_used`, the attributed and unattributed work, work per class and per logged operation, the 20 most expensive calling lines, and every row. Unattributed work was zero in every run below: every unit of the request counter was charged inside a profiled decider call. The profiled runs reproduced the release binary's work exactly (999,689, 999,970 and 999,665 units at 1M; 2,727,172, 9,149,520 and 6,501,409 at 100M).

Classes:

| Class | Decider calls |
| --- | --- |
| Exact-zero sign | `sign` that returned `ProvenCoincident` by rational arithmetic, an algebraic method or a separation bound |
| Unseparated enclosure | `enclose` whose final ball still contained zero |
| Nonzero sign | `sign` proved by the interval filter, refinement or exact nonzero arithmetic |
| Enclosure | `enclose` that separated zero, `enclose_at`, `enclose_all_at`, `enclose_budgeted_at` |
| Root isolation | `real_roots`, `certify_root` |
| Checkpoint | `check_deadline`, one unit per cancellation checkpoint |
| Exact reduction | `rational_identity`, `rational_value`, `angle_degrees` (zero work in these cases) |

The first two classes bound what any bounded 1 nm decision could remove. Even that bound is generous: a bounded decision still needs a certified enclosure of the residual and a scoped conversion to a distance. For exact-zero signs the profile also records the work up to the first refinement enclosure; only the remainder is avoidable in principle.

### What this means for the 1 nm rule

The three budget refusals are not caused by exact-zero proofs. Under PLAN's refusal-repair sorting they are growing complexity (the crossing-pipes root isolation and the seam coefficient enclosures) and repeated or slowly converging evaluation (the arctangent checkpoints in STEP export). Their fixes belong to refusal repair, not to the tolerance core. The 1 nm rule remains required for its own reasons in PLAN item 11, but it is not a speed lever for these cases. No `NotExact` decision occurred in any of the runs.

## 2. The inventory

### Sources and method

`tools/tolerance-inventory/not_exact_sites.py` lists every non-test source line that names `NotExact` (132 at this commit, one of them the profiling classifier) and every `Outcome` match, `matches!` or `let` form without a `NotExact` arm (73), where a wildcard, binding or `else` arm receives it. The scan is textual. Reading each site found further consumers it misses, listed in section 2.5. Each listed site was read for its role, the question it decides, what `NotExact` does there today, the call-site fan-in of small helpers, and what a 1 nm decide-once rule would need there.

Tolerance-rule categories:

- **(a)** A candidate for 1 nm sameness: a typed conversion from ARCHITECTURE's table exists or can be written, and the rule would turn a refusal into an answer. Each still needs the whole-effect proof and a decide-once record.
- **(b)** Keep refusing or declining: ordering, orientation, branch or component choice, strict positivity, algebraic structure, exact-correspondence gates, measurement, export and display accuracy. A tolerance cannot answer these.
- **(c)** Unsafe today: `NotExact` silently or visibly selects geometry or output without a scoped proof. These must be fixed before the policy is declared enforced, whatever else lands.

Totals over the 197 kernel rows, excluding the profiling classifier (some rows merge arms split across lines): 1 producer, 8 re-producers, 11 passthroughs, 165 consumers and 12 type or documentation rows. 22 rows are (a), three of them only once `same` checks every curve component; 151 are (b), 8 are (c) and 16 make no decision. Two further (c) paths, `hits.rs:1283` and `:1333`, are `Failure::Uncertain` arms the scan does not list. Several (a) rows are wrappers whose individual callers split between (a) and (b). These counts are at `35fe6bf`. All ten (c) paths are now fixed, so no row is (c); [section 2.7](#27-rescan-at-9b31177) records the rescan at `9b31177`.

### 2.1 The producer, re-producers and passthroughs

`Decider::sign_inner` is the only constructor of `NotExact` in `catallax-numbers` (`crates/catallax-numbers/src/decider.rs:1033`). It stops with `numbers.budget-exhausted`, `numbers.precision-limit` or `numbers.operation-budget` and keeps the magnitude of its tightest ball, a unitless residual bound. `Decider::compare` and `Outcome::map` pass it through. `Decider::enclose` counts an unmet width as `not_exact` in its statistics but returns a ball, not an `Outcome`. `real_roots` and `certify_root` never return `NotExact`, so the arms that handle it after those calls are dead.

| Site | Role | What happens to the bound |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `crates/catallax-shapes/src/store.rs:445` `side_of` | re-producer | `Surface::distance_bound` turns the residual into a distance to the carrier surface, not to a trimmed face. BSpline carriers have no conversion and refuse. The Torus, Cone and EllipticCylinder formulas need a theorem review before use. |
| `crates/catallax-shapes/src/store.rs:813, 825` `decide_zeroes` | re-producer | Keeps the raw bound per equation and converts with the caller's distance bound. For `same_point` that is a true distance. For `Curve::Intersection` it is the larger of two carrier distances, not the distance to the curve. |
| `crates/catallax-intersect/src/query.rs:243, 253` `on` | re-producer | Divides by a divider-norm lower bound: distance to the branch divider, not to the branch. Line 253 replaces the witness's bound with the point's own divider distance. |
| `crates/catallax-intersect/src/query.rs:274, 291, 300` `equal_dimensions`, `equal_points`, `equal_lines` | re-producer | Valid distance conversions: square root of a squared difference, and point-to-line distance for lines already proven parallel. |
| `crates/catallax-intersect/src/query.rs:161` `surface_domain` | re-producer | Divides by the cone-axis norm; consumed by `keep_branch` (refuses) and `on` (passes through). |
| `crates/catallax-intersect/src/query.rs:409, 438, 468, 501, 537` `same` | passthrough | Returns the first unresolved component's bound (apex, radius, semi-axis, focal parameter) without checking the remaining components. The returned bound does not bound the gap between the curves. |
| `crates/catallax-intersect/src/query.rs:217` `on` | passthrough | Returns the carrier result unchanged and skips the cone-domain, component and divider checks. |
| `crates/catallax-intersect/src/math.rs:27, 69` `Failure::outcome`, `sign` | passthrough | Wraps and unwraps raw sign bounds; about 107 call sites. `From<Failure> for Refusal` (`math.rs:19`) refuses with the numbers code. |

Consequence: the `bound` in an `Outcome<Apart>` from `same_curve` or `on_curve` can be a true distance, a distance to a carrier or divider, a partial component bound or a raw residual, and `NotExact` carries no unit or scope to tell them apart. Piece 1's typed evidence must replace this before any consumer reads the bound as a gap.

### 2.4 Structural findings

- **Evidence-discarding wrappers.** `catallax-features/src/profile.rs:43` (about 500 calls) replaces every `NotExact` with `features.precision`, dropping code and bound. `catallax-boolean/src/geometry.rs:42, 51` keep only the detail; `catallax-features/src/end_condition/curved.rs:211`, `catallax-field/src/end_condition.rs:25`, `catallax-field/src/profile.rs:33`, `catallax-measure/src/junction.rs:79` and several STEP sites substitute domain codes. Budget exhaustion is then indistinguishable from unsupported geometry, and a decide-once rule cannot be layered on these wrappers without changing them first.

Status (M12): every refusal built from a `NotExact` now keeps it (`Refusal::undecided`), with its `numbers.*` code and bound, at all of the wrappers above and at every site in section 3 that refuses with `n.code`. Wildcard arms that refuse with a piece code (`lib.rs:346` `face_contains_with`, `stages.rs:187`, `face_checks.rs:336`, `certified/junction.rs:504`, `measure/bounds.rs`, the STEP pipe, saddle, junction and torus-band clearance sites, `rolling_junction.rs:492`) keep it through an explicit `NotExact` arm or `Refusal::keeping_undecided`. Refusal remaps (stage codes, `named_pair`, `fillet.invalid-edge`, field envelope codes, mesh face prefixes) recode in place and keep it too. Codes and details are unchanged, so no saved answer changes. Where an undecided identity test reads as "not equal" and a refusal follows, the refusal now keeps the first such decision: `measure/src/junction.rs:79`, STEP `junction.rs:94` and `frames.rs:142, 199-207`, `features/src/cone_build.rs:119` and `circular_build.rs:106`, and `topology/src/rolling_mitre.rs:26` through `RollingMitreBand::checked` for its refusing callers. - **Code-dependent control flow.** A `boolean.*-ambiguous` refusal derived from `NotExact` is caught and retried (`points.rs:830-837`, `region.rs:517`, `face_queries.rs:60`); a `numbers.*` refusal from the Bézier probe helper aborts the whole witness search. `catallax-step/src/partial.rs:34-37` retries on every code except `numbers.operation-budget` and `numbers.deadline`. `catallax-step/src/band.rs:176-190` falls back to the legacy charted layout whenever the band route declines, so the written layout can depend on the work allowance. Status (M12): the band route, the chart frame search (`chart.rs:45`) and the cone axial-ring test (`partial.rs:677, 680`) refuse an undecided test instead of declining, and neither the equator-split retry (`partial.rs:34`) nor the exporter's charted retry answers a refusal that keeps an undecided decision or carries a work or precision code (`partial::allowance_bound`). A smaller allowance now refuses or writes the same layout; the last digit of a written decimal can still differ with the precision limit, which is export accuracy, not layout.
- **Dead arms.** `boolean/src/stages.rs:220`, `boolean/src/sewing.rs:539`, `features/src/planar_shell.rs:502` (`classify_point` never returns `NotExact`), `numbers/src/real_poly.rs:400, 666`, `shapes/src/rational_sheet.rs:1180`, `shapes/src/rolling_pipe/line.rs:309` and `intersect/src/solve.rs:266`. ## 4. Decide-once merging against the saved answers

### Question and answer

M12 allows decide-once merging only at the 22 category (a) places in section 2.3, and only where it changes an answer for the better. A saved answer can improve only by turning a refusal into an answer, and only if that refusal comes from an undecided decision at one of those places.

None qualifies. No saved answer makes an undecided decision. Of the 17 that run out of work, the three where exact-zero proofs dominate spend that work in ordering and orientation tests, category (b), which a tolerance cannot answer. Step 2 of M12 is closed with no merging. The three cases are filed under refusal repair below.

### Method

The pinned 477-question replay corpus was run at its 100M allowance with 8 workers, using the binary at `main` with the second evidence group applied; raw evidence is retained privately. Each manifest's `stats.not_exact` counts undecided decisions. A request that runs out of work is replaced by a whole-request `numbers.operation-budget` refusal without statistics, so those 17 were profiled again with the `work_profile` example of section 1 at the same allowance. For the three dominated by exact-zero proofs, a local build marked the boolean `sign` wrapper `#[track_caller]` to attribute its work to the calling line; that change is not committed.

### Refusal repair

These go to refusal repair, not to the tolerance rules:

- **Edge-germ ordering zero proofs (replay 330, 377 and 378).** `germ.rs:319` (`half`) and `germ.rs:125` (gradient parallel test in `curvature`) prove exact zeros algebraically at about 0.9 to 2.5M units per call, between 76% and 92% of the 100M allowance in these three cases. A cheaper exact route is needed: a construction fact that the tangent lies on the reference or that the gradients are parallel, or an exact method that does not grow with the expression. The answer must stay exact; a tolerance does not apply to an ordering.
- **The other 14 budget refusals.** These are dominated by imprint coefficient enclosures (`probe_path.rs:32`), root isolation (`solve.rs:266`) and measurement checkpoints (`integrate.rs:81`), the same growing-complexity and slow-evaluation classes as section 1.
