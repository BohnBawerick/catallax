# Numerical STEP for exact bodies without an exact STEP form

An ordinary exact recipe request now answers with an approximate STEP file when the exact writer refuses `step.unsupported-geometry` and the body has a numerical embedding. This follows the ROADMAP "Numerical first" rule. The tolerance is the exact writer's default 1e-7 mm curve tolerance, fixed before the attempt. Construction, embedding, numerical admission, writing and the file reread share the request's unchanged allowance.

`catallax_step::numerical::build_from_exact_model` keeps the retained topology, planes and native rational spline carriers, including multi-chart atlas faces written as their single source surface. Patch-section and patch-pair edges become fitted cubics over a monotone world coordinate, or over their traced pieces with C0 joins when no coordinate is monotone. Every edge use on a spline face receives a fitted native UV curve at that edge's parameter. Binary64 arithmetic only proposes samples and fits. The existing numerical admission rechecks every edge, trim and loop at 128 bits, and the numerical writer rechecks its decimal payload. This mirrors OCCT's `GeomInt_IntSS` approximation of a section as one 3D curve and one pcurve per face.

The manifest keeps its exact-request format with `exact: false`, a `step.numerical-approximate` mark and an approximate `step` object. Its fields are listed in [the wire contract](../../VERIFY-INTERFACE.md#numerical-step-output). No complete body bound is claimed. Bodies outside the embedded family keep the exact refusal and its work.

Numerical admission now accepts rational atlas faces, evaluated through their retained source surface. That source can hold other components, so every edge use on an atlas face needs a native UV curve, and every sampled UV location must lie on one of the face's own charts (`trim.sampled-atlas-chart-union`). The charts must also fill their parameter box exactly (`surface.atlas-charts-fill-box`), so a loop whose boundary lies on them encloses only chart points; a face whose charts leave a gap refuses `numerical.unsupported-carrier`. The numerical writer emits the source surface and partitions its rounded copy again for the emitted check. Binary64 copies of knots, controls and weights are charged by size before conversion.

The seven recorded M10 requests that construct their result (M10-001 to 003, 007 and 012 to 014) now write approximate STEP at 20M. `tools/step-regressions/test_m10_spline_boolean.py` checks the approximate answers and the remaining refusals.
