## 2026-11-21: the number types count their own arithmetic

Pieces: 1 (numbers), and every piece that runs ball or rational arithmetic outside a decision; judge interface (`catallax-verify` responses).
Change: `catallax_numbers::OperationWork::uncharged` now holds every ball and rational operation a request runs outside a decision, priced at the decider's rate: a ball sum or difference 4L, a ball product, square, reciprocal or square root 8L^2 with L = ceil(p / 64), a rational sum or difference its operands' limbs added, a rational product or quotient their limbs multiplied, a decimal conversion the value's limbs squared, and one unit more for each. `Ball` and `Rational` count themselves as they run. `catallax_numbers::count_exact_arithmetic` is removed, and so is the `counted` module of `catallax-step`. `catallax_numbers::uncounted_memo` is new. `Decider::reserve_exact_arithmetic` takes what it charged off the count, because the operation it pays for counts itself. Nothing is charged: `used`, `exhausted`, the allowance, every refusal and every answer are as before.
Why: Checkpoint 2 decision Q13, predictor step 1 ([section 4.5](../checkpoints/checkpoint-2-decisions.md#45-item-30b-one-model-and-the-predictor)), and its done-when line "wall time per million work units stays within a 10x band across all rows". The count of the curve fit alone covered 37% of STEP export time. The owner ruled on 21 Nov 2026: make the meter complete, count every ball and rational operation, charge nothing. Method and limits are in [the milestone note](../milestones/3.0B-predictor.md).
Migration: delete calls of `count_exact_arithmetic` and write the arithmetic plainly; it is counted where it runs. A request's cost at the kernel's rate is still `used + refunded + uncharged`. A test that pinned an `uncharged` value pins a larger one. Code that fills a memo on a value shared between threads or requests, where the fill is not charged and runs ball or rational arithmetic, wraps the fill in `uncounted_memo`, so that the count does not depend on which thread or request filled it. A memo whose fill is charged keeps its `shared_caches()` guard and needs nothing new.
