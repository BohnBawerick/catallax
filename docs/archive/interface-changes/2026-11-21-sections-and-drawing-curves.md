## 2026-11-21: plane sections face by face, and drawing curves

Pieces: application (`catallax_app::queries`, `catallax_app::views`).
Change: `section_curves(model, body, plane, dec)` loses its `Accuracy` argument and no longer refuses a plane through a planar face with `app.section-coplanar`. It now cuts each face on its own, as OpenCASCADE's `BOPAlgo_Section` does against an unbounded plane, instead of intersecting the body with a cutting block, so it no longer measures a bounding box. `SectionCurves::curves` holds `SectionArc { curve, start, end, faces, edge }` in place of the Boolean engine's `SectionEdge`: every edge of the body that lies in the plane comes first, once, under its own id, then the cuts across faces in face order; a cut that runs along an edge in the plane is that edge. `faces` lists the face a cut crosses, or the faces of the edge. `points` also holds vertices that touch the plane off every curve. A curved face that coincides with the plane refuses with `app.section-coincident`.
New in `catallax_app::views`: `drawing_curve`, `edge_drawing`, `face_drawing_loops` and `stroke_drawing` turn a carrier between two points, an edge from its start to its end, a face's loops in travel order, and a hidden-line stroke into `DrawnCurve { curve: DrawingCurve, fit }`. `DrawingCurve` is `Line`, `Arc` (counter-clockwise about its unit normal, from its start point at angle 0), `Circle` or a clamped `Nurbs`, all binary64. Lines, circles, ellipses, parabolas, hyperbolas and B-splines keep their exact shape to binary64; intersection branches, patch sections and patch pairs are fitted cubic B-splines with a `ContourFit`, and the answer carries a `DRAWING_FIT_MARK` (`app.drawing-fitted`) mark. A whole line, parabola or hyperbola, or a carrier with no conversion, refuses with `app.drawing-curve`.
Why: milestone 3.1 needs exact section curves and exact drawing geometry for SolidDraft's `section_curves`, `edge_curve`, `face_loops` and `view_curves`, which use display polylines today. A drawing section often lies in a face of the part.
Migration: drop the `Accuracy` argument from `section_curves` and `ApplicationBody::section_curves`. Read `faces` where code read `faces_of_first`; `faces_of_second` has no counterpart, since there is no cutting tool. Code that expected `app.section-coplanar` now receives the edges in the plane.
