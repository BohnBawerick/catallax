## 2026-11-21: one pruned gap between two solids, and clear answers that state a wide gap

Pieces: measure (`catallax_measure`), shapes (`catallax_shapes::distance`), application queries (`catallax_app::queries`)
Change: `catallax_measure::solid_gap` returns the minimum distance between the closed boundaries of two solids that do not cross. It visits face pairs in order of their boxes' separation and stops once the best distance is no greater, as OpenCASCADE's `BRepExtrema_DistShapeShape` does. `body_relation` calls it in place of its own loop over every face pair, and it no longer runs the overlap Boolean on two solids whose proven boxes are strictly apart. A clear answer whose gap enclosure is wider than the caller's accuracy now carries the `measure.accuracy-not-reached` mark instead of refusing `app.relation-gap`; that code now means only that the gap is not proven positive. New helpers: `catallax_shapes::distance::face_distance_below`, `PreparedFace::face_to_face_below` and `PreparedFace::parallel_witness`. The entity distance search also answers a line parallel to a circle's plane in closed form, and two parallel planar faces without exact rational frames from their own edges and vertices.
Why: roadmap 3.3 asks for clash, clear by a stated gap, or unresolved for every pair. Distant pairs refused `measure.distance-uncovered` on circle edges far from the minimum, or ran out of the 100M allowance in the Boolean. Turned bodies refused `measure.irrational-plane`. The Checkpoint 2 rule keeps proven, bounded and refused answers separate, and a marked enclosure is the bounded kind.
Migration: callers that treated every `BodyRelation::Clear` as meeting the requested accuracy must read the answer's marks: a `measure.accuracy-not-reached` mark means the stated gap is proven but wider than requested. `app.relation-gap` now always means a gap not proven positive. Every pair that answered before answers the same enclosure and marks. `PreparedFace::parallel_face_to_face` is unchanged.
