## 2026-10-25: complete circular cylindrical distance joins

Pieces: features, field and comparison

Change: outward offsets of convex coaxial plane/cylinder/sphere cells retain complete circular source trims and directly assemble exact parallel faces and torus normal sectors. Spherical caps keep their native carriers. Closed shells and one smooth cylindrical or spherical band's finite normal image use the same definitions. Source face names survive; edge tubes use `round` names and sweep ends use `rim` names.

The independent field constructs normal images and complete edge tubes from its own supports. Spherical band limits are cones of constant polar angle. Closed named-face queries prove tangent rail ownership through transverse trim dominance and exact full-tube enclosure, while retaining intermediate Boolean ownership. Buried tangent donors and mixed-polarity source paths do not acquire a direction from one witness.

Why: enlarged sharp cylindrical supports are not true-distance joins. At their smooth tangent rails, a tangent-plane sign test also cannot distinguish an exposed patch from an isolated buried contact.

Migration: no recipe, exchange format, geometric tolerance, output accuracy or work allowance changes. Noncoaxial, nonconvex, partial, oblique and horn/spindle joins retain typed coverage refusals. Sharp multi-face thicken is unchanged. Existing full-ring property integrals measure covered new faces without adding measurement work to part construction.

Prior art: [OCCT arc joins](https://occt3d.com/dev/doc/refman/html/class_b_rep_offset_a_p_i___make_offset_shape.html) distinguish face parallels, edge pipes and vertex spheres. [SMLib's rolling-ball sheets](https://smlibpy.classcad.ch/docs/surfaces/) and [Patrikalakis, Maekawa and Cho's pipe-surface discussion](https://web.mit.edu/hyperbook/Patrikalakis-Maekawa-Cho/node210.html) separate carrier construction from local and global trimming. These distinctions motivate the finite normal-domain and complete circular-tube proofs here; no source code is copied.
