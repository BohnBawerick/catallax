## 2026-10-25: bounded cylinder-join distance features

Pieces: features, field; consumers: topology, measurement, display, STEP and comparison.
Change: Add `catallax_features::cylinder_join::CylinderJoin` and its complete-band/plate attachment witnesses, with bounded outward-offset and single-face normal-sweep dispatch in both evaluators. Convex circular joins use exact torus sectors, concave roots use intersected analytic parallels, and the rectangular plate retains the existing rounded-box cylinder and sphere sectors.
Why: A boss attachment leaves a circular hole in the plate face, and a stepped shaft leaves an annular shoulder; neither finite domain can be replaced by an unbounded displaced support or a full cap disk.
Migration: Existing recipe formats, callers, names, accuracy controls and work limits are unchanged. Shared-definition callers must supply complete source trims: this lane covers a fully contained perpendicular boss on a rectangular plate, or two consecutive unequal-radius coaxial bands with concordant carrier axes; the field recognizer currently requires a two-primitive union. Strict radial, axial, shoulder and plate clearances exclude new contacts. One selected smooth wall, circular disk/annular shoulder or attachment plate face has a finite normal image; new sharp multi-face selections and general trimmed/spline or noncoaxial/oblique envelopes remain unsupported. Default field mass and native boss-offset display remain unfinished at their unchanged resource limits, as recorded in [the cylinder-join ledger](../CYLINDER-JOIN-LEDGER.md); sampled comparison is not a whole-result distance certificate. No fitting, tolerance, budget or opt-in elliptic-prism carrier changes are introduced.
