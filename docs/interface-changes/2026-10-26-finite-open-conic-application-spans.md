## 2026-10-26: finite parabola and hyperbola application edge spans

Pieces: application (`catallax_app::curves`)
Change: `CurveSpan` adds `OpenConicArc { interior, first, last }`. `edge_curve` answers a finite edge on a `CurveForm::Parabola` or `CurveForm::Hyperbola` carrier with an exact interior point from `arc_point` and the exact transverse coordinates of the two endpoints that come first and last along the carrier's natural direction, as ordered by `order_on_curve`. The coordinate is measured from the parabola vertex or hyperbola centre along the unit of `normal x axis_dir` and is strictly monotone on the arc. The carrier kind, branch, edge sense, trim endpoints in edge order, origins and construction pairs stay on `EdgeCurve` as in [0170](0170-2026-10-25-exact-trimmed-application-edge-queries.md). An open conic edge without paired finite bounds, with coincident endpoints, or whose endpoints are not in carrier order refuses with `app.curve-trim`; the message of that refusal now names open conics as well as lines. Intersection-query refusals and work allowances propagate unchanged. Line, circle and ellipse answers are unchanged, and splines and general branches stay `Unsupported`. This is a geometric query, not drawing visibility or silhouette support.
Why: finite parabola and hyperbola edges of represented bodies were reported as `Unsupported` although their exact carrier and trim are known.
Migration: callers with an exhaustive `match` on `CurveSpan` add the variant; callers that treated parabola or hyperbola edges as `Unsupported` read the new span.
