## 2026-10-27: STEP import body entry `import_step_bodies`

Pieces: `catallax-import`, recipe evaluation of `import_step` (not yet wired), piece 3 read-only
Change: `catallax-import` adds `read_step(bytes) -> StepFile` (exact recognised faces, edges, vertices in millimetres), `build_file` / `build_solid` (topology into a caller's `Model`, faces named `{step, imported: {key: "#n"}}`) and `import_step_bodies(model, bytes, expected_sha256, step) -> Result<Vec<BodyId>, ImportRefusal>`, which checks the saved hash first. Follows [the wire types](2026-10-27-step-import-repair-wire-types.md); there `apply` and `propose` still arrive later.
Supported: planes, cylinders, 45 degree cones, lines and circles, several solids, seam edges (dropped from the model, cylinders get ring loops as Catallax's own primitives do). Refused by entity name: spheres and tori (pole and seam boundaries), ellipse edges, other cone angles, B-splines, voids, shell models, assemblies. Every edge must lie exactly on its faces and every vertex on its edges in exact arithmetic; otherwise `not_solid` and nothing is moved. Vertices join only by exactly equal coordinates and straight edges by their joined vertices.
Why: the importer was missing; a recipe step needs a fixed imported body that rebuilds identically from the saved file bytes.
Migration: None yet. Wiring `Operation::ImportStep` into `catallax_features::evaluate` needs a file source the evaluator does not have today (`evaluate(recipe, dec)` takes no bytes); that belongs to the features/app lanes and is requested in the pull request.
