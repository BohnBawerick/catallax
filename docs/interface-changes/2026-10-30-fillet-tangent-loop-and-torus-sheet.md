## 2026-10-30: tangent fillet loops and the outer torus sheet

Pieces: 2, 4, 5, 6, 7 (catallax-shapes `Surface`, catallax-features `fillet`/`fillet_build`, catallax-intersect, catallax-boolean, catallax-measure, catallax-mesh, catallax-step)
Change: `Surface::TorusSheet(TorusSheet)` and `SurfaceKind::TorusSheet` add the outer sheet of a horn or spindle torus, `0 < major <= minor`. It covers the points with `major + minor cos v > 0`. Its side is that of the outer tube, `(rho - major)^2 + z^2 - minor^2`, and it has no global polynomial, so `Surface::implicit` returns `None`. An elementary plane/cylinder fillet whose wall contact lies away from the axis now uses this sheet when `0 < major <= radius`; `FilletDefinition::surface` can therefore be a `TorusSheet`. Two strips whose edges and outer supports are tangent at a vertex now join on their shared meridian circle, with no corner face. Line strips whose complete carriers meet are accepted when their cross-section kites are disjoint. In `catallax-recipe/4`, a selected edge continues across smooth tangent boundaries along its chain, and the continued strips keep its blend name. Version 3 selections stay local.
Why: Rounding the top boundary of an extruded slot needs the four-edge loop at the application default radius 4. There the end-cap contact is part of a spindle torus with major radius 1 and minor radius 4. The exposed quarter keeps a distance of at least 1 from the axis, so it is regular, but the `Torus` ring invariant cannot hold it.
Migration: Existing ring tori are unchanged and `Torus::new` still refuses `minor >= major`. Consumers treat a sheet face through the ring torus `(u, v)` chart only for face trims, boundary integrals, meshing and STEP pcurves, where every point already lies on the sheet. Curve hits use the complete carrier and keep only the hits on the sheet. A hit at the carrier's self-intersection refuses with `intersect.singular-surface-point`. Surface pairs, distance features, parallels, IGES and field fillets keep typed refusals. The field fillet keeps the earlier `fillet.unsupported-contact` refusal. The CLI manifest reports sheet faces as `torus`. STEP export now also charts partial toroidal faces that are trimmed along their outer equator from above. It writes those sheets as `TOROIDAL_SURFACE` with the stored radii.
