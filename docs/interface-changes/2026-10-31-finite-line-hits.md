## 2026-10-31: finite line crossings of a rolling envelope

Pieces: 2, 3, 4, 5, features
Change: `SplineSupport` gains `line_hits`, and `RollingEnvelope` gains `line_hits` and `boundary_circle`. `intersect_curve_surface` now answers a whole line against a rolling envelope with its complete, unique, exact transverse crossings of the finite carrier, each `Contact::Crossing` and unmarked, in line order. `envelope_retains_chart` in piece 3 says whether a face keeps the envelope's whole chart. `classify_point` and `face_contains` use that, with the recorded hit, to place a ray hit on such a face, so a fitted P5 body answers native containment for points whose rays cross a fitted patch. Finite spline query enclosures now stay at or below the caller's precision, and fresh query enclosures are charged to the caller. A stored 128-bit net is rounded outward, never refined.
Why: strict native containment refused on every ray that met a fitted patch, and a fresh 128-bit evaluation could run under a lower caller ceiling.
Migration: a caller that matched `intersect.parametric-surface` for a transverse line against a rolling envelope now gets the answer. A tangency, such as a line on the rail cylinder, a contact the enclosures cannot separate, and a line in the mouth plane keep that refusal. `CurveSurface::Disjoint` after this path is still a proved miss. An elliptic offset support keeps its refusal for any line it cannot prove clear. A face that does not retain the whole chart keeps its trimmed-domain path. No fitted surface, fit target, tolerance, recipe format, writer or manifest changes.
