## 2026-10-31: P5 fillet rail tangent to its countersink mouths

Pieces: features, boolean, measure, step, recipe contract
Change: a lone closed concave plane/cylinder fillet ring now answers when its plane rail touches countersink mouths at isolated points, instead of refusing with `fillet.unsupported-overflow`. Each contact is one vertex shared by the rail and the mouth circle. The result keeps one exact torus and no marks. A short straight seam beside each contact cuts a cap off the plane, so `faces_by_kind` counts one extra plane per contact. This is a bounded exception to the ND6 maximal-face rule, recorded in RECIPE.md section 1.4. A closed loop of arcs on one circle now has ring membership on a cone and closed-form integrals on a cone or torus. The STEP writer seams a torus that ends in such a loop, and the cones and cylinders beside it.
Why: rail and mouth are coplanar circles outside each other at the sum of their radii, so they meet at one point and neither carrier gains a false surface tangency. Without the caps the plane would pass through each contact twice, and no consumer here has a contract for that loop. The closed forms need no boundary trace, which the rotated placement could not afford within its allowance. Without the seams the raw read of the file was invalid.
Migration: a caller that matched the refusal for P5 radius 1 now sees an exact manifest/3 record. Plane faces of that body are not all maximal: read the flange top as a set of patches sharing `housing.side(flange-top)`, never a single face handle. Radius 7/2, a tube too short to carry the rail, a mouth the ball rolls onto beside a tangent one, and a tangent mouth without a narrowing cone keep their refusals. No recipe format, fitted surface or tolerance changes. Faces outside this family keep their answers and STEP bytes.
