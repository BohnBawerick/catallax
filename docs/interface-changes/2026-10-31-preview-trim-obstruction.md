## 2026-10-31: Construction preview refuses a buried capsule carrier early

Pieces: field, app
Change: `catallax_field::display::preview_plan`, and so `catallax_app::construction_preview`, can return the new refusal `field.display-trim-obstruction` before any cell traversal. It names the capsule side and the cap it crosses. No mesh or partial preview comes with it. `mesh_plan`, the certified route, never runs this check.
Why: a capsule's semicircular walls constrain the display through complete cylinders. The unused half of such a cylinder changes sign in every cell it crosses, so a cell on it never proves stable, and where the result has no boundary within twice the tolerance no mesh vertex can cover it either. Refinement then spends the allowance and refuses, whatever the allowance. The check proves that case exactly for a stadium profile on a planar support and leaves everything else to the existing traversal.
Migration: a caller that matched `numbers.operation-budget` or `application.preview-incomplete` for such a body now sees `field.display-trim-obstruction` at a fraction of the work. A direct `preview_plan` caller receives this refusal where it used to get a mesh with `unresolved_cells` above zero. The slotted SolidDraft bracket's refusal costs 17,519 work under the unchanged 2,000,000 reservation. Bodies without a stadium profile do no extra work. Near-capsule profiles that are not exact stadiums keep the traversal and its current cost.
