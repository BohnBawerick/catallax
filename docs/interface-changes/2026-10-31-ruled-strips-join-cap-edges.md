## 2026-10-31: ruled display strips join multi-edge caps

Pieces: display mesh

Change: a one-loop cylindrical or elliptic-cylindrical face bounded by exactly two straight rulings now meshes as one ruled strip when a cap between the rulings joins several edges, as on a slot end wall whose top arc is cut by a straight fillet strip. Each cap must still be one strictly monotone angular chain from ruling to ruling, every triangle must still pass the existing chord, edge-length, aspect and normal certificates, and any failure still returns the face to the periodic chart triangulator. Unproved previews split one-loop ruled faces at the same rulings and join the curved runs between them.

Why: filleting one straight top edge of an extruded slot ([the cylindrical end-wall cut](2026-10-30-fillet-cylindrical-end-walls.md)) leaves each end wall with five coedges. The four-coedge strip test sent both walls to the chart triangulator. Its centroid refinement in the turn-by-millimetre chart made about 60,000 triangles per wall at the default 0.01 mm chord. Under SolidDraft's default 15 degree normal limit, triangles pressed against the walls' constant-height arc rails failed the normal test at every size, so every accepted radius from 0.25 to 4.9 mm refused with `mesh.precision-or-budget` at `max_vertices`, and a 60 degree limit exhausted the 20M application allowance. The preview refused or exhausted the allowance on the same walls.

Migration: none. A four-coedge certified face takes exactly the earlier path. A four-coedge preview face whose two curved coedges are adjacent now uses the chart triangulator, which keeps the vertex splitting its ruling; no test or target part has such a face, and no other face in the tests or target parts takes the new path. The slot fillet's end walls now have no interior vertex, and every accepted radius builds and meshes closed in at most 0.36M application work at 0, 15, 30 and 45 degrees, and 1.9M at 60 degrees. No API, exact geometry, picking ID, tolerance, vertex limit, allowance, manifest or STEP output changes.
