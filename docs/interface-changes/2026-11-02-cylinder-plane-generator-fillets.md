## 2026-11-02: cylinder and parallel plane fillet strips

Pieces: features, field, application, verification
Change: B-rep fillets now admit straight generator edges between a circular cylinder and a plane parallel to its axis, when the offset line and offset circle cross and the existing end and trim proofs succeed. The strip is an exact cylinder. The field now evaluates the same case with a section that follows the cylinder's arc, when the section is rational and the supports share one sharp generator. For a strip with a cylinder support, another face on the same support no longer returns `fillet.unsupported-overflow`, and two strips that consume one band of a shared plane now return it, as they already did on a shared wall. Opt-in operation logs record `fillet.contact`, `fillet.sections` and `fillet.trimming` for this case.
Why: a flat on a cylinder, a boss through a wall and a bore breaking through a wall are common, and they reduce to one offset line and one offset circle in a normal section. Neighbouring faces on the same plane are normal here: a boss through a flat splits the flat in two. The field's straight section would have changed the material set, so it needed curved support boundaries before this case could be enabled. Planes that are not parallel to the axis, nonparallel cylinders, curved edges and rollover stay refused.
Migration: no recipe schema or function signature changes. Select the wall and the plane with an ordinary `between` reference. Without `near` it chooses every generator between those faces, under RECIPE.md section 3.4. Callers comparing both kernels must expect `field.fillet-contact` when both generators of a pair are selected, and a refusal for an irrational section. Consumers of operation logs must accept these phases on the new case. Parallel-cylinder fillets stay field-refused.
