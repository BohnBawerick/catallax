## 2026-11-02: volume, STEP and face measurement for oblique fillet bands

Pieces: shapes, topology, measure, field, step, verification, application
Change: a whole `SplineSupport::RollingPipe` band now answers `volume`, `mass_properties`, `face_area`, `face_centroid`, `bounding_box` and `face_bounding_box`, and `write_step` writes it as a rational surface with its shared ellipse rails and a seam. `measure_face` gives zero against a `FaceDefinition::Fillet` whose `CentrePath::Arc` is the whole centre ellipse, and the radius gap against any other radius on that ellipse. `RollingPipe` gains `law`, `centres_on`, `enclosed_controls` and `axis_extrema`, the private law is now the public `PipeLaw`, and `catallax_topology::pipe_outward` tells which way a band faces. `pipe_ring` accepts a closed edge with a vertex. A band whose trim is not its two whole rails refuses measurement with `measure.unsupported-surface`. STEP refuses with `step.unsupported-geometry` when two bands share a wall but need different seam frames. The field still refuses these recipes with `fillet.lost-edge`. The `catallax-verify` recipe adapter reports such a face under the new `rolling-pipe` surface tag in manifest/3, where it used to refuse with `numbers.geometric-gap-unproven`.
Why: this is the third piece of the plane and oblique cylinder slice. A rounded rim, boss or bore can now be measured and exchanged, so a caller no longer has to warn that these solids stop at display.
Migration: code that warned that volume and STEP refuse these solids should drop the warning and check for the two refusals above. A caller that matched the old `measure.unsupported-surface` or `step.unsupported-geometry` answer for a whole band now gets a result instead.
