## 2026-11-02: rolling-ball fillets on oblique plane and cylinder ellipses

Pieces: shapes, topology, boolean, mesh, features, application
Change: the recipe `fillet` operation now answers a closed `Curve::Ellipse` edge between a plane and a cylinder with one named band face on `SplineSupport::RollingPipe`, a new variant. `FilletDefinition::new` builds the band and both rails. `RollingPipe` gains `is_edge`, `clear_of`, `line_hits`, `side_at`, `normal_at`, `on_boundary` and `display_grid`, and `catallax_topology::pipe_rows` reads a band's two rings. Point classification and the mesher accept these faces. A band reports its angular control as unavailable. Volume refuses with `measure.unsupported-surface`, and STEP output, `measure_face` and the field refuse. A neighbouring curved face reaching the band's hull refuses with `fillet.unsupported-overflow`.
Why: this is the second piece of the plane and oblique cylinder slice. It puts the exact carrier from the previous piece into solids, so a rim, a boss and a bore on an oblique face can be rounded and shown.
Migration: code that matches `SplineSupport` exhaustively needs a `RollingPipe` arm. A recipe that used to refuse such an edge with `fillet.unsupported-contact` now builds, so a caller that relied on the refusal must check the result instead. Warn that volume and STEP refuse these solids until the next piece lands.
