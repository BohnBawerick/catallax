## 2026-11-02: fillets on a closed ring between nonparallel cylinders

Pieces: features, shapes, topology, boolean, mesh
Change: a fillet on the closed `Curve::Intersection` ring between two nonparallel cylinders now builds a named `RollingSaddle` band when the narrow cylinder passes cleanly through the wide one. `FilletDefinition::ring` builds that definition from the ring's witness point. `FilletDefinition::edge` can now hold a `Curve::Intersection`, and `FilletDefinition::contacts` a `Curve::BSpline`; `transform` keeps that edge as it is, since its ids only name the ring and the moved geometry is in `supports` and `surface`. `RollingSaddleDefinition` gains `through` and `runs_with_chart`. `RollingSaddle` gains `clear_of` and `extent`. `catallax-topology` gains `saddle_rows`. A saddle's mirrored copy now answers `line_hits` correctly: the solver rebuilt `across` from the moved axes, and a reflection turns their cross product round. A face bounded by a spline curve now takes the traced boundary's winding where a surface probe used to refuse with `intersect.parametric-coincidence` or `intersect.parametric-curve`. The mesher's closed bands share one routine for seeding their rails. A trimmed cylinder face now meshes as a lattice of straight columns under a triangulation that measures both parameters in turns, so it has far fewer triangles, and `FaceMesh` and triangle lists for those faces differ from earlier versions. A cylinder trimmed by another cylinder no longer runs out of vertices under an angular limit.
Why: this is the third piece of the nonparallel cylinder slice, which puts the fitted carrier into a solid.
Migration: code that reads `FilletDefinition::edge` or `contacts` must accept these variants. Volume, measurement and STEP of a body with this band refuse with `measure.unsupported-surface` and `step.unsupported-geometry`. A body box refuses with `measure.unsupported-surface`, so does a pattern or a kept mirror of such a body, and a boolean on one refuses with `intersect.unsupported-pair`.
