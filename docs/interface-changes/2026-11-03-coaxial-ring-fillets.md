## 2026-11-03: fillets round circles between coaxial surfaces of revolution

Pieces: shapes, features, intersect, mesh, step, SolidDraft's Catallax adapter
Change: a fillet on a circle where two surfaces of revolution about one axis meet now builds an exact torus strip, where it refused with `fillet.unsupported-contact`. Supports are planes normal to the axis and cylinders, cones, spheres and tori about it, in any pair other than a plane and a cylinder, whose strips are unchanged: frustum rims, both edges of a chamfered rim, countersink mouths and throats, dome rims, seated spheres, revolved grooves and capped rods. The edge must be a `Curve::Circle`; a circle held as an intersection curve, such as a plane through a torus, keeps its refusal. The new law is `catallax_shapes::RollingTorus`: `RollingTorus::new(supports, sides, edge, radius, dec)` returns the torus, or the outer sheet of a horn or spindle torus when its centre circle is no wider than the ball, and both contact circles; `RollingTorus::radius_limit` gives the radius past which no ball rests on both supports, except beside a cone. Its refusals are `shapes.rolling-torus` and `shapes.rolling-torus-edge`, which `FilletDefinition::new` reports as `fillet.unsupported-contact`, `fillet.radius-too-large` with its limit, or `fillet.invalid-edge`. New refusal details under `fillet.unsupported-contact`: "the ball centre reaches the axis" and "a contact reaches the axis". The torus axis points so that a band ending on its outer equator lies on the axis side of it.
Consumers changed with it. `catallax_mesh` retries a cone or torus band with finer rows when a row beside a rail misses the chord bound, which it previously handed to the general triangulator. A line along a torus's axis now meets it by two quadratics, so point classification near these strips answers. `write_step` writes a torus band at any tube angle beside a cone, starting its seam on its low ring, and cuts a band across its torus's outer equator into two export-only faces. An outer torus sheet beside a cone refuses with `step.unsupported-geometry`. The field kernel still refuses these rounds with `field.fillet-contact`.
Why: slice 5 of the general fillet plan covers curved edges and periodic seams beyond the cylinder pair. Every surface of revolution has a line or circle as its profile, so the ball centre is where two offset profiles cross, and the strip is exact: no fit, no certificate, and a zero deviation from the rolling-ball definition.
Migration: code that treated these edges as refusals sees a body with one more face per selected edge, whose `Built::fillets` definition holds a `Surface::Torus` or `Surface::TorusSheet` and two `Curve::Circle` contacts. SolidDraft needs no new call: pin this revision, keep showing `fillet.unsupported-contact`, `fillet.unsupported-overflow` and `fillet.radius-too-large` as before, and expect `step.unsupported-geometry` from STEP export of a narrow cone rim's outer torus sheet. Other fillet results, refusal codes, tolerances and work allowances are unchanged. Meshes of cone and torus bands that the band route used to give up on, and STEP files of scenes the writer used to encode without seams, change; every other mesh and file keeps its bytes.
