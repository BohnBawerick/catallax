## 2026-11-03: a fillet radius proved too large for its edge has its own refusal

Pieces: features, app
Change: a fillet now refuses with `fillet.radius-too-large` where it proves that the requested radius cannot fit the edge, in place of `fillet.unsupported-contact`, `fillet.unsupported-overflow`, `intersect.*` or another unfinished-work code. There are two proofs. In the first, no ball of the radius rolls along the edge against both faces: it does not fit inside a cylinder it must sit in, or the offsets of the two supports no longer cross the way the edge does. In the second, the ball's contact with one face lies wholly outside that face, past a sharp boundary. When the proof has a bound, the detail ends with `; radius limit <decimal> mm`. Every radius above the exact bound is too large, and the decimal is that bound rounded down to three places. `catallax_features::fillet::RADIUS_TOO_LARGE` names the code, and `catallax_features::fillet::radius_limit` and `catallax_app::Error::fillet_radius_limit` read the bound as a `Rational`. A radius at the bound itself, where the ball path pinches or degenerates, keeps its earlier refusal.
Why: a user asking for an impossible radius saw an unfinished-work refusal, which SolidDraft shows as Coming soon or as a generic message. The proofs cover plane and plane, plane and cylinder rings and ellipses, straight generators between a plane or cylinder and a parallel cylinder, and the ring or loop where two nonparallel cylinders cross. A proof only relabels a refusal the construction already returned, so nothing that built before refuses now. The recipe's section 4.5.1 still defines a roll-over result for these radii. This code means it does not fit the edge's own faces, not that the recipe's geometry is impossible.
Migration: code that matched `fillet.unsupported-contact` for an over-large radius sees `fillet.radius-too-large`. SolidDraft can show "The radius is too large for this edge." and, when `fillet_radius_limit` is `Some(x)`, "Try a radius under x mm." Other fillet refusals are unchanged.
