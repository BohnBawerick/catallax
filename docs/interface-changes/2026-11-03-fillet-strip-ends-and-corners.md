## 2026-11-03: fillet strip ends and corners build, and unfinished ones say which they are

Pieces: features, field, app
Change: more fillet ends and corners build, and every end or junction case not built yet refuses with its own code.

- A straight strip between planes is checked against the body's other faces only between its two end faces, not along its whole cylinder. Edges of pockets, steps and bosses that refused with `fillet.unsupported-overflow` ("a non-end neighbour intersects the blend carrier", or "nonincident blend carriers meet") now build, in both evaluators.
- Three equal concave strips between independent planes, such as a pocket's floor corner, close with an exact `corner.vertex` sphere sector, as three convex strips did. They refused with `fillet.unsupported-corner`. The sector face is reversed: the material lies outside the ball. The field adds the corner cell where it subtracts a convex one.
- Two straight strips of one convexity sharing a face meet at a vertex where their outer faces lean differently, such as a sloped wall's two edges at a corner. The deeper strip runs past the other's outer rail and is cut by that rail's face, an exact step with one more vertex and edge. This refused with `fillet.unsupported-topology` ("unequal endpoint contacts").
- An arc strip ends at a meridian circle on a planar end face through its axis, such as the arc top edge of a half-disc or a half-disc pocket's floor. This refused with `fillet.unsupported-topology` ("vertex trimming needs straight rails").
- A straight strip ends on a spherical end wall along their exact intersection, as it did on a cylindrical one. This refused with `fillet.unsupported-topology`.
- A ring round whose ball sits inside its cylinder, such as a cylinder's bottom edge or a blind pocket's floor, keeps its torus axis pointing from the ball centre to the plane contact. The same torus wrote unreadable STEP faces before; the point set is unchanged. `Built::fillets` holds that axis.
- New refusal code `fillet.unsupported-end` (`catallax_features::fillet::UNSUPPORTED_END`) for a strip end not built yet: an arc strip on an end plane off its axis or on a curved end face, an oblique-ellipse band cut open, a straight strip on an end face other than a plane, cylinder or sphere, and one strip ending at a vertex of more than three faces. `fillet.unsupported-corner` (`catallax_features::fillet::UNSUPPORTED_CORNER`) now covers every junction not built yet: convex and concave strips at one vertex, curved strips meeting at a sharp vertex, two strips of unequal reach across an edge of the other convexity, and three strips without an exact sphere sector, and two or more strips at a vertex of more than three faces. Both replace `fillet.unsupported-topology` for these cases; `fillet.unsupported-topology` remains for edges and names the fillet cannot resolve.
- New `catallax_features::fillet_corner::SphereSector`: the sphere sector of a ball touching three supports, from its centre, radius, three unit contact directions and material side, with its boundary great circles, dual basis and sector test. `CornerDefinition` now holds one as `sector`, in place of its `normals`, `radius`, `surface` and `feet` fields; `centre`, `boundary` and `in_sector` are unchanged. New `FilletDefinition::cut_hull` and `catallax_features::fillet::hull_clear` bound a straight strip's region between two cutting planes.

Why: the release needs fillets on ordinary pocket, step and boss edges and their corners, and SolidDraft needs to tell an unfinished end or corner apart from other unfinished fillet work. Junctions of equal crossing cylinders need the same corner patch, so `SphereSector` is public.
Migration: SolidDraft sees results where it saw the refusals listed above. It can show "Fillets ending here are not supported yet." for `fillet.unsupported-end` and "Fillets meeting at this corner are not supported yet." for `fillet.unsupported-corner`; code that matched `fillet.unsupported-topology` for an end or corner matches these instead. Callers of `CornerDefinition` read `corner.sector.directions`, `corner.sector.radius`, `corner.sector.surface`, `corner.sector.feet` and `corner.sector.convex`.
