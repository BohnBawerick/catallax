## 2026-11-03: hole ring fillets roll on the material side

Pieces: features, SolidDraft's Catallax adapter
Change: a fillet on a circular edge between a plane and a perpendicular cylinder now takes its convexity from both faces at a point of the ring, as straight, elliptic and crossing-cylinder edges already did. A through-hole mouth is convex and its torus lies outside the bore; a blind-hole or counterbore floor ring is concave and its torus lies inside. Before every build, each ring strip's two contact rails must leave the edge into their own faces, or the step refuses with the new code `fillet.contact-off-face`. The concave-ring envelope continuation now declines a ring whose ball is inside the wall, so such a ring keeps its original refusal. The radius-limit proofs no longer skip circular edges, since the construction's convexity is now the two-normal test they checked: `convexity_agrees` is gone.
Why: the old test read only which side of the circle the plane face lies on, so it treated every inward wall like an outward one. Catallax v0.1.0 through v0.1.2 built an R1 fillet on the mouth of an R3 hole as a torus of major radius 2 instead of 4, and a blind floor as 4 instead of 2. The wrong strip put each rail beyond the face it replaced. Loops still closed and the shell stayed oriented, so no structural check refused it.
Migration: a recipe that rounds a hole mouth, a blind-hole floor or a counterbore ring now gets the correct solid, or a refusal where no correct ring exists: a floor round wider than the bore refuses with `fillet.radius-too-large` and the bore radius as its limit. Boss tops and boss feet are unchanged. SolidDraft must pin this revision and show `fillet.contact-off-face` like any other fillet refusal. No recipe format, public type, tolerance or work allowance changes.
