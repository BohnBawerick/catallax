## 2026-11-03: a fillet on the loop of two crossing cylinders builds a solid

Pieces: features, shapes, mesh, measure, intersect, boolean, step
Change: a fillet on the one closed edge where two nonparallel cylinders cross in a loop now builds a body with a fitted band, where it refused with `fillet.unsupported-contact`. The edge is a loop when the narrow cylinder runs past the side of the wide one, so it winds round neither. The body classifies points, meshes and answers `bounding_box`. `RollingSaddleDefinition` gains `crossing`, which picks the through ring or the loop, and `runs_with_chart` now answers on a loop. `RollingSaddle` gains `display_columns`, the chart parameter of each grid column. `TracePiece` gains `enclose`, a box over a parameter interval that follows a spline span's controls, and face membership uses it. On a loop, `volume`, `volume_area_centroid` and `mass_properties` refuse with `measure.unsupported-surface`, `write_step` with `step.unsupported-geometry`, and `certify_junction` with `numbers.geometric-gap-unproven`.
Why: this is the first solid piece of the loop slice. The loop's carrier and queries landed earlier. Its volume, file and certificate integrals read a ring that winds round the narrow cylinder, so they refuse until the next piece rewrites them. The interval evaluation of a spline span's polynomial overstated a rail's box by orders of magnitude, so face membership beside a rail ran out of sampling cells.
Migration: code that treated this edge as a refusal sees a body. Its band reports `SaddleRing::Overlap` in `law().ring`.
