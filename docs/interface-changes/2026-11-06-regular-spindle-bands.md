# Complete regular horn and spindle bands

STEP's complete-band encoder now handles a native `TorusSheet` bounded by
two full coaxial circles, beside the existing cylindrical and conical bands.
It proves positive boundary radii and an oriented tube-angle interval that
avoids the inner equator. The writer requires radial clearance and distinct
seam endpoints beyond twice its composed rounding bound. Uncertain cases refuse without
publishing partial geometry. Trimmed spheres and arbitrary cone charts keep
their existing routes.

This makes the unchanged `narrow-cone` fillet export at 1M. It does not add a
constructor, carrier or recipe field. Digits remain 17 and file tolerance
remains 0.0000001 mm. General quaternion placement can still refuse during
construction. The oblique-cylinder volume comparison and attribution remain
open. The complete 43-case local report is required for every published head;
hosted smoke is separate evidence.
