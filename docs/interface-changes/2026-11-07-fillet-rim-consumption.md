## 2026-11-07: exact closed-rim face consumption

Pieces: features, topology, intersection, naming/history, whole-face measurement, STEP.

Change: a single closed convex rim of a complete capped cylinder now follows its finite contact interval through face consumption. The input proof identifies the stock radius `R`, height `H`, two full circular planar caps and outward wall, in any rigid placement. The first event is `L=min(R,H)`. At `L=R` the centre circle contracts and its exposed sectors form the hemisphere towards the selected cap. At `L=H<R` the opposite cap terminates the wall contact and the retained band is an exact torus or regular outer horn/spindle sheet. The cap is consumed at `R`, the wall at `H`, and both when they coincide. No zero-length edge or zero-area face is published.

Why: inside the cylinder, closest support distance is `R-s` with `s>=0`, so no original contact spine exists beyond `R`. At `H` a continued centre would leave the opposite edge's inward normal cone; its flat cap has no curvature fold to trigger the wider-ball cliff rule. The affected regions are nested, so their union remains consumed when the last admissible branch ends. A request beyond `L` retains that exact terminal region only after the complete finite-component proof. This is not a size heuristic, signed-radius fold or retry with a guessed radius. Before the event, the same finite proof also resolves ordinary bands whose untrimmed toroidal carrier reaches the opposite cap while their exposed quarter remains above it.

Intersection: a regular outer torus sheet requires strictly positive radial distance. Its quartic carrier also has axis nodes which are not points of that support. Curve/surface intersection now filters those carrier-only roots instead of refusing an unrelated finite-sheet query as singular. Actual outer-sheet crossings remain exact hits. Material regressions cover rational axis queries and an algebraic carrier node lying inside the trimmed solid.

Measurement: the exact whole-face circle-distance certificate applies to regular horn/spindle sheets as well as ring tori. Positive radial distance makes the nearest centre-circle distance equal to the tube radius over the entire support. The certificate checks centre, axis and major radius independently and measures the absolute difference from the target radius; it does not include the folded quartic branch or infer agreement from a display mesh.

Names: surviving input faces keep their origins; the band or hemisphere keeps the original `blend.edge`. Canonical `consumed` records identify only retired input faces, coalescing identical source sets without aliasing vanished names onto replacements. Disconnected unselected components retain their whole point sets: every affected region is contained in the proved cylinder component, and input lump interiors are disjoint.

Interface: `FilletDefinition.radius` remains the radius of its exposed contact sheet. New `requested_radius: Option<Real>` preserves a larger authored request when that sheet ends before the requested homotopy endpoint. `None` means the sheet reaches the declaration; callers needing the declared value use `requested_radius.as_ref().unwrap_or(&radius)`. Constructors `new` and `ring` initialise `None`; Rust callers constructing the public struct directly must add that field. No recipe syntax or schema changes. Existing `ConsumedFace` history also records these retired cylinder faces; its fields are unchanged.

Independent whole-recipe field parity is not claimed by the separate analytic whole-face comparisons.
