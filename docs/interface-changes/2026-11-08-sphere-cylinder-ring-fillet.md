## 2026-11-08: Ring fillets where a cylinder passes through a sphere

Pieces: 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, judge interface
Change: a fillet on the closed `Curve::Intersection` ring where a cylinder passes through a sphere off its centre now builds a body with a fitted band, where it refused with `fillet.unsupported-contact`. `catallax_shapes` gains `RollingSphereDefinition`, `RollingSphere` and `ROLLING_SPHERE_TARGET_NM`, and `SplineSupport` gains the variant `RollingSphere`. The band is a closed rational carrier of degree (8, 2) with its cylinder rail exactly on the cylinder and its sphere rail exactly on the sphere through a stereographic chart, a recomputed two-sided bound under the 100 nm target and a regularity proof. Its queries match a through-ring `RollingSaddle`'s, including `line_hits` when its frame and rows are rational, except that it has no display grid. It also has `parameter_table` for both rails. `FilletDefinition::ring` builds it from the ring's witness. A cylinder that does not pass cleanly through the sphere still refuses with `fillet.unsupported-contact`, and a coaxial pair keeps its torus. `saddle_rows` and `saddle_added` accept the band, with the sphere rail first. `JunctionCertificate` gains `sphere_bands`, which returns the new `SphereBandRegion`. The manifest/4 certificate gives such a face the role `rolling-sphere-band` with `sphere-rail` and `cylinder-rail` rows, its `retained_target.kind` is `rolling-ring-junction` with `sphere_band_faces` and `saddle_faces` when a step rounds a sphere band, and `spline` lists each band. `write_step` and `write_rolling_junction_step` write the band with one seam, with the sphere and cylinder kept exact and their charts turned about the cylinder's axis. `volume`, `area`, `centroid`, `mass_properties` and `bounding_box` refuse such a body with `measure.unsupported-surface`, and meshing it refuses.
Why: the sphere-cylinder corpus case refused, while OCCT builds a blend whose volume is 2.8e-4 mm^3 from the exact one.
Migration: code that matches `SplineSupport` exhaustively needs a `RollingSphere` arm. Readers of the manifest/4 certificate should accept the new role, rows and target kind.
