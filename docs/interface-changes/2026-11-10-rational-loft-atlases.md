## 2026-11-10: exact rational lofts and logical chart atlases

Pieces: 2, 3, 6, 7; features, application and display mesh

Change: Version 4/5 `Operation::Loft` reaches the B-rep evaluator and Catallax application entry with its existing ordered section/start-id fields and ruled/smooth law. Exact common-denominator refinement and uniform natural interpolation retain positive rational spline definitions; no approximation or numeric-policy change is introduced. Version 3 arc-length lofts retain `loft.legacy-parameterization` rather than changing their saved meaning.

The ordinary recipe verification entry uses the existing `catallax-manifest/5` vocabulary when the result retains a checked loft cell cover. This admits exact spline surface/curve kinds without inventing a fitted-geometry tolerance certificate. Analytic reductions keep the ordinary analytic exchange.

Known application limit: the three-circle natural lofts at heights `[0,1,2]` and `[0,2,5]`, with radii `[1,2,1]`, return `ResourceLimit` at the unchanged 20M eager-build allowance. Their explicit refusal tests remain in the suite; deterministic-work profiling is a separate follow-up. This slice does not claim default-budget completion for those two cases.

Piece 2 adds `RationalAtlas` and `AtlasChart`, with a complete regular chart cover, exact oriented G1 transitions and finite union queries. `SplineSupport::RationalAtlas` is appended to the existing variants. The retained source tensor can cover more than one maximal logical component; component queries use the chart union, not that tensor's whole rectangle. Local chart proofs do not constitute global body admissibility. Off-carrier joined-atlas side queries retain a typed parametric-query refusal.

Exact subspan/subrectangle homogeneous extraction and positive homogeneous curve/surface constructors share the existing spline machinery. `BSplineCurve::joined` joins complete directed pieces by exact endpoint identities and positive homogeneous normalization, without fitting or moving the curve.

Piece 3 adds `AtlasFaceGroup`, checked `coalesce_rational_charts`, and read-only `ChartExpansion`. Coalescing checks the exact complete chart faces and their prescribed sewn transitions. It removes numerical seams from published topology, combines contributing origins and joins physical boundary curves. `Model::chart_expansion` and `Model::face_chart_cover` expose the separate exact cells to consumers; those cells are not additional selectable faces, edges or vertices. Isometric copies transport both the logical model and its exact cell cover. Exact positive-control-hull checks reduce planar components and straight boundaries to analytic supports. `ChartExpansion::needs_cells` distinguishes consumers that still need numerical charts from fully reduced analytic bodies.

Display remaps certified cell triangles to logical face keys, retains only physical edge polylines and composes the existing error/control reports. Certified affine parameter transfers between identical spline boundary rows avoid per-vertex inverse solves. Interval jets and their independent-column projection bounds are reused only within the certified operation enclosure; directional refinement avoids doubling an irrelevant parameter axis. No work allowance, mesh cap or tolerance is raised. Body/face integration and geometric bounds operate on the exact retained cover when needed. STEP may split a logical face into file charts: `StepReport::face_map` names the logical model face, while added-edge/vertex counts include the export-only chart topology. No file displacement is charged to the exact model.

Why: A single globally injective rectangular patch cannot represent a periodic loft side or a general connected collection of exact loft charts. Exposing every numerical cut as a model edge would change selection and names.

Migration: Existing recipe fields and entry signatures do not change. Matchers that need joined logical spline geometry must handle the appended support variant or preserve a truthful typed refusal. Numerical consumers should use the checked model cover rather than treating the source tensor as the finite domain of every component. Existing spline constructors and old support discriminants retain their meaning. See `docs/LOFT-CASES.md` for operation cases, sufficient global embedding certificates and remaining legal-input coverage gaps. The field evaluator and SolidDraft command/conversion layer are unchanged.
