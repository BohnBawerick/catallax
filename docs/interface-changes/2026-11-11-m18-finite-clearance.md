concave boss-foot ring can now prove its closed radial band disjoint from the
entire finite native round primitive owning a candidate face. Strict separation
uses exact placements and sign predicates; touching or intruding bodies retain
the refusal. See [proof and limits](../M18-FINITE-CLEARANCE.md).

This admits the public P5 radius-1/2 edit, including translated and rotated
recipes. Authored P5 radius 2 still requires native rolling-envelope region and
transition coverage. Section 4.17 smooth blend is not implemented. Existing
successful paths, tolerances, allowances and saved inventories are unchanged.
