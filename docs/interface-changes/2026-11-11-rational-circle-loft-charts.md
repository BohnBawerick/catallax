## 2026-11-11: rational circle loft charts and the natural-circle budget cases

Pieces: 3; features, application and display mesh

Change: On a common loft parameter interval where every section's chart is a rational quadratic, the loft carrier applies one exact Möbius reparameterization to all sections when exact field reduction proves every resulting coordinate rational and every weight positive. Full-circle quarter charts with weights `1, cos 45°, 1` become rational charts with weights `1, 1/2, 1/2`. A loop whose charts are then all rational carries a running rational homogeneous scale across its intervals instead of raising the u degree. The solid, its faces, names and station curves are unchanged; the exported spline controls and the u speed of those faces change.

This corrects the known application limit in [the rational loft atlas entry](2026-11-10-rational-loft-atlases.md). The three-circle natural lofts at heights `[0,1,2]` and `[0,2,5]` with radii `[1,2,1]`, and the two-circle loft with radii `[1,2]` at heights `[0,3]`, now build with display inside the unchanged 20M eager application allowance. Their outcome changes from `ResourceLimit` to a built body.

Why: The algebraic quarter-circle controls made every enclosure and exact-zero proof walk expressions over `√(1/2)`. The three-circle loft operation alone exceeded 4G work units. See [the work count](../LOFT-CASES.md#where-the-20m-allowance-goes).

Migration: Recipe fields, entry signatures, refusal codes, allowances, tolerances and display caps do not change. Consumers that compare exported loft spline controls or face parameters for circular sections must expect the reparameterized carrier; positions on the surface are unchanged. Intervals that mix circular charts with other chart kinds keep their previous algebraic carrier.
