## 2026-11-12: a fitted blend's area encloses its carriers

Pieces: 6 (measurement), 1 (shapes)
Change: on a body whose fitted faces are all rolling envelopes that keep their whole chart, `volume_area_centroid` and `area` under the represented scope state an area enclosure that contains both the exact definition's area and the represented body's, and carry no `measure.area-of-definition` mark. The enclosure adds each carrier's certified area less its envelope's exact area (`BSplineSurface::certified_area`) and, for each rail, the strip it can move on the cylinder: at most the fit bound times 2 pi times the cylinder radius. Bodies with rolling saddles or sphere bands, or with an envelope face trimmed off its chart, keep the definition's area and the mark. `BSplineSurface::area_enclosure` and `certified_area` now share their cells among the kernel's threads and sum them in a fixed order, so the enclosure is the same at any thread count.
Why: PLAN M12 asks for area, volume, centroid and STEP of bodies with fitted faces each to carry its own stated bound. The mark said the area did not cover the carriers; for whole-chart envelopes it now does.
Migration: readers that looked for the mark on envelope-only blends find none; the area enclosure there is wider than before by the carriers' and rails' contribution. Volume and centroid are unchanged.
