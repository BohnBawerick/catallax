# Bounded sign on the caller's decider

`catallax_numbers::Decider::sign_within` certifies a sign under a work cap no
larger than the decider's own policy. The decision keeps that decider's
deadline, statistics, caches and request allowance, and a caller policy with a
smaller `max_work` keeps its smaller limit. `numbers.budget-exhausted`
identifies a decision that spent the narrowed cap; `numbers.deadline` and
`numbers.operation-budget` remain operation-wide stops.

Piece 4 uses it for the parallel cone/torus minor-angle quarter sign, so k13
stops below the 20M application allowance while the caller's deadline,
statistics and bounded decision evidence survive the call. Pieces 1, 4 and 5
must keep passing those hard stop codes through contextual refusals rather
than relabelling them.

Piece 5 raises `boolean.parametric-query` for an exhausted imprint only at the
fitted spline query that spent the allowance, naming the observed curve and
face kinds. Every analytic exhaustion keeps `numbers.operation-budget`, even
when the graph carries fitted faces elsewhere.
