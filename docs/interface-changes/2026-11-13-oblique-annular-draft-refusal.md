# Oblique annular draft coverage

An oblique cylindrical draft on a hollow coaxial source now returns
`draft.unsupported-oblique-annulus`. The former `draft.unsupported-oblique-neutral`
described the axial cone constructor, without identifying the hollow arrangement.

The refusal names the missing whole-height, whole-angle radial separation proof
and shared trimmed cap rings. It records enclosing intervals for the source
outer and inner squared radii, cap span along pull, authored angle tangent and
squared transverse-to-axial neutral-normal ratio. It also records which walls
were selected. `certified_radial_gap=unknown` means that no fitting-gap bound has
been proved. The source radius difference is not a certificate for the drafted
material.

B-rep and field construction share this diagnostic after checking the complete
source annulus. Selecting the exterior wall, the bore wall or both has the same
coverage refusal. Zero-angle draft and the supported axial annular laws retain
their construction path. Invalid source arrangements and numeric exhaustion
retain their own refusals.

No recipe version, modelling tolerance, work allowance or geometric definition
changes. Hollow oblique construction remains parked until the two radial laws,
their separation and their common cap trimming can be certified together.
