# Draft and shell with mixed through openings

B-rep Draft and Shell accept a convex planar block with separated circular bores and convex polygonal through openings between the same two perpendicular caps. The opening inventory matches both physical cap wires and their prismatic side walls before filling the outer envelope. It rejects a mismatched or nonconvex void rather than filling an unproved hole.

Draft can tilt the outer side planes while keeping these openings fixed. Each polygonal void must remain strictly inside the drafted side supports. Its original edges, cap wires, wall surfaces and origins are sewn into the result alongside the preserved circular bores.

Shell uses the existing convex cavity and circular bore offsets. For strictly separated openings, it sews the vertical faces of each convex rounded void offset directly into shared cavity cap wires. Common axial translations move the two rows into the cavity caps. Circle centres identify those rows without algebraic endpoint zero tests. Strict support margins prove separation from the cavity sides and other openings. The general offset/Boolean path remains available when those checks do not establish separation. Within the cap interval both constructions give the distance envelope of the physical side walls, including circular corner sectors. Offset planes carry inner origins and corner cylinders carry round-edge origins. The cap-only and single-side opening selections use the existing finite-face checks.

The construction uses exact planes, cylinders and circles. STEP serialization retains its separate file tolerance and accuracy report. No field-kernel parity or timing qualification is implied.

Property queries can use an equivalent rational circle frame when the construction reference has irrational length. Cylindrical generator edges contribute zero to the existing boundary forms, including those with algebraic origins. Circle arcs retain their oriented swept interval. The regular-opening regression checks every face area, centroid and bounding box, every edge length and midpoint, and full mass properties needed by the editor.

Public fixtures `draft-mixed-openings.json` and `shell-mixed-openings.json` use a 40 by 30 by 12 block, a radius-3 bore and a six-sided through cut. `shell-regular-openings.json` retains the decimal coordinates captured from SolidDraft's regular-polygon tool, including its small floating-point asymmetries. Raw STEP regressions compare independent section volumes and material witnesses, including points that distinguish rounded polygon corners from mitered corners. Operation logs separate opening inventory, shared cavity construction, general offset/cut work and final material subtraction.
