# Shared stock-ring parameters

`hole::cylinder_ring_curve` generates a numerical cylinder pcurve for a retained quadratic circular ring fragment. The caller supplies the original 3D curve and finite parameter interval, the actual `NumericalCylinderSource`, its shared boundary row and preset tolerance. The mapper selects the matching cylinder quarter from the complete original source-span endpoints. It preserves the 3D curve, native parameter and finite edge interval.

Opposite native angular frames cannot use linear reversal for the existing asymmetric circle weights. A fresh control using the actual radius-12 stock source measured a 0.100417531 mm mismatch on the upper retained ring family. The lower same-frame control matched. For the sliver-side fragment used by the next join, linear reversal misses its endpoint by about 0.002113 mm at diameter 2 and 0.00866 mm at diameter 1.5.

The generated pcurve has degree one and positive weights. Let a and b be the middle/start weight ratios of the source quarter and target cylinder row. Its second weight is a/b for matching directions and 2ab for opposite directions. Its endpoints are the target native U corners and the selected constant V row. The knot interval remains the source's original complete span. The mapper checks endpoint ownership and 17 same-parameter 3D/surface compositions over the finite ring fragment, under the caller's tolerance. Positive weights give a continuous parameter hull inside the selected quarter; no whole-geometry deviation bound is claimed.

`CylinderRingCurve` retains a caller key, target quarter, unchanged native UV definition and numerical evidence. It grants no exact incidence, complete shell admission or STEP authority. `None` identifies a source that has no single corresponding native quarter for its finite interval.

The next skew-collar join reuses the existing component from https://github.com/BohnBawerick/catallax/pull/661. Its original upper stock ring, sliver-side root, existing cone/tool section and one shared component vertex identify the anchor at diameters 2 and 1.5. The stock pcurve and existing cone/tool native maps compose to that same vertex within 1e-7 mm; keys and pcurve/evidence transfer are checked. The actual incoming Cylinder/Cylinder section remains required before full-join acceptance. No new cap or broad capture is added for this control.

The approach follows OCCT 7.9.3 [BRepLib::SameParameter](https://github.com/Open-Cascade-SAS/OCCT/blob/V7_9_3/src/BRepLib/BRepLib.cxx), which adjusts and checks pcurves against an unchanged 3D edge. P04's SMLib map records SmCrvOnSurf evaluation and NL_CrvReparamToInterval in curve_on_surface.rs and curve_compat.rs. The latter rescales knots affinely. This caller uses a fractional native map for the retained circular sources and checks its surface composition; it adds no intersection fitter.
