## 2026-11-18: exact fillets first, refunded on refusal, then the rolling ball

Pieces: 1; features, application, import reader and display mesh; SolidDraft adapter
Change: `catallax_numbers::refund_on_refusal` runs an attempt whose refusal returns its work charges and clears an exhaustion it latched; a success keeps every charge. `catallax_features::evaluate_incremental_outcome` tries the exact fillet builders through it and hands any refusal of theirs to the rolling-ball construction. `catallax_app::build_incremental_without_display` returns the exact `EvaluatedApplicationBody` as before; `build_incremental_with_files_without_display` returns `EvaluatedApplicationOutcome`, exact or numerical. The rolling ball takes an `overflow` of `roll_over` and a `corner` of `cliff` as the defaults they are; `keep_neighbours` limits and a `smooth` corner refuse `features.numerical-fillet-closure`. A fillet neither construction builds keeps the exact builders' refusal: its code, and its detail followed by `; numerical continuation refused <code>: <detail>`. A radius limit is the exception: one the rolling ball proved is reported as `fillet.radius-too-large` with `exact path refused ...; numerical continuation refused ...`, and one only the exact builders proved keeps that code with the rolling ball's cause first; either way a terminal `; radius limit X mm` stays terminal. The STEP reader accepts a `TRIMMED_CURVE` whose parameter trim is exactly the domain of its B-spline basis with agreeing sense, as the numerical writer emits for native spline edges; every other trim stays `Unsupported`. `catallax_mesh::preview_step` halves a curved cell along the axis it bends along and keeps curved cells from growing far longer than wide. It no longer compares the normal of a triangle so thin that moving a corner by the chord target tilts it past the angular bound, refines a triangle no longer than the chord target on account of its normal alone, or tests the middle of a triangle side that lies on a trim, which the edge sampler has already placed within an eighth of the chord target. A carrier sample off its triangle by more than the chord target is refined whatever the triangle's size, as before. The exact builders also bound a strip's finite region (its hull, a closed ring's whole sweep, a corner's contact cone) against a trimmed neighbour, but only where the whole carrier meets that neighbour's surface or the pair has no intersection answer, so a fillet that built before costs the same work.
Why: sending every fillet to the rolling ball made box fillets approximate and refused every operation after a fillet. The owner's pocket rim, which the exact builders refuse, needs the rolling ball with the whole allowance, and its body has to display and export through the numerical STEP file.
Migration: callers that want numerical fillets call `build_incremental_with_files_without_display` and match the outcome; callers of `build_incremental_without_display` change nothing. Refusal codes of fillets the exact builders refused are unchanged, except where the rolling ball proves a radius limit. Code that reads a radius limit must take it from the terminal `; radius limit` suffix or `catallax_features::fillet::radius_limit`. Preview triangle counts on cylinders, cones and tori fall; no certified mesh changes.
