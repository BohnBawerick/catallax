## 2026-11-20: numerical admission measures each edge use

Pieces: 3, 5, 7, features, import, cli, app (every caller of `catallax_topology::numerical::build*`, `rebuild*` and `sew`)
Change: the `trim.edge-surface-distance` and `trim.numerical-pair-edge-surface-distance` records of a numerical body now hold one sample per edge use: the parameter of the largest gap found by [`numerical::gap::measure`](2026-11-20-edge-gap-checker.md) and, as `distance_upper_mm`, the tolerance stored for that use, 1.1 times the measured gap plus its measurement error (rule T4). Before, they held nine equally spaced samples for an ordinary trim and the eighths of every knot span for a native UV curve, each with a 128-bit distance at that location. `NumericalOptions::tolerance_mm` now limits the stored tolerance of an edge use, so a gap between 10/11 of the limit and the limit refuses where it passed. The number and names of check records per body, and every other check, are unchanged. A face copy that leaves a spline surface's domain refuses with `numerical.invalid-trim`; two new codes can reach a caller, `numerical.gap-unsupported` and `numerical.gap-work-cap`.
Why: roadmap item 3.0A. A fixed sample set reads a gap only where it samples. On the known-answer edges it found between none and 99.7% of the true gap, and one existing test body carried a hole edge 2.7 mm off its side face between the nine sample locations. The measurement also costs hundreds of times less work than the 128-bit samples.
Migration: read `samples[0]` of an edge-surface record as "largest gap, with margin" and do not expect nine or more samples. A body that now refuses with `numbers.numerical-tolerance-exceeded` has an edge whose true largest gap, times 1.1, is above the limit; fix the fit or state a larger limit, do not look for a sample to move. See [the checker note](../milestones/3.0A-edge-gap-checker.md).
