## 2026-11-20: tolerance rules T0 to T8 and the export receiver profile

Pieces: topology, features, import, step, cli (every piece that admits or exports a numerical body)
Change: New public module `catallax_topology::numerical::tolerance` with the numbers of rules T1 to T8 and their checks: `floor_mm`, `BodyFrame`, `own_fitted` and `FittedTolerance`, `import_cap_for`, `pair_verdict`, `grew`, `growth_warnings`, `ReceiverProfile`, `feature_band`, `features_within`, `largest_recorded`, and the report types `ToleranceReport`, `EntityTolerance`, `EntityKind`, `ToleranceWarning`, `FeaturePair`. `NumericalBody::tolerances()` returns the report admission now builds: every vertex and edge tolerance over rule T1's floor, and the warnings of rules T1, T5 and T7. Admission refuses a pair of entities that share no topology when their tolerances add up to more than half their distance, with the new code `numerical.tolerance-pair` (rule T5). Every continuation compares its result with its input and refits the face copies of an edge that rose more than ten times (rule T7). New `NumericalBody::refit_face_copies` and `RefitRow`; new `numerical::admit_import` (rule T3: refit before refusing, opt-in up to 0.1 mm, refusal `numerical.import-opt-in`), which numerical import now admits through. The numerical import's caps accept an opt-in up to 0.1 mm instead of refusing above 0.01 mm. `TolerantBody::cap_mm` gives a vertex 0.02 mm at the automatic cap. The numerical fillet evaluation and the CLI's numerical export of an exact body fit to rule T2's target (1e-6 mm under 10 m) and admit under its cap (1e-5 mm), where both were 1e-7 mm; the loft cut fits and admits at the target. `NumericalStepOptions` has two new fields, `receiver: Option<ReceiverProfile>` and `scale: Option<LengthScale>`, and `NumericalStepReport` three: `uncertainty_mm`, `receiver: Option<ReceiverReport>` and `scale`. A numerical STEP export with a receiver profile writes a global uncertainty, the largest entity tolerance; without one the header is unchanged. The numerical body handover layout is `CXNUMB04`, which adds the report; `CXNUMB03` and `CXNUMB02` bodies read back with an empty report.
Why: roadmap item 3.0A, decision Q7 (section 4.3 of the Checkpoint 2 decisions). See [the tolerance rules note](../milestones/3.0A-tolerance-rules.md).
Migration: struct literals of `NumericalStepOptions` need the two new fields or `..Default::default()`. Code that reads a numerical STEP header written with a receiver profile must accept a `GLOBAL_UNCERTAINTY_ASSIGNED_CONTEXT`, and, for a scaled export, a length unit other than the millimetre. A caller that relied on admission accepting two entities closer than twice their tolerances gets `numerical.tolerance-pair`. A caller that passed a numerical import cap above 0.01 mm to be refused now gets an admission at that cap up to 0.1 mm.
