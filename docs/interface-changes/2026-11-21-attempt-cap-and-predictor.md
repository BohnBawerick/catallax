## 2026-11-21: a capped exact attempt, the predictor and the fence before the exact Boolean

Pieces: 1 (numbers), 3 (topology), 5 (boolean), 7 (features), and the application's build through `evaluate_incremental_outcome`
Change: `catallax_numbers` gains `capped_attempt(run)`, which runs an attempt whose charges are returned on a refusal as `refund_on_refusal` does and which may be given a cap; `cap_attempt(units)`, which sets that cap from inside the attempt; `cap_attempt_when_reached(first, decide)`, which lets the attempt charge `first` units and asks `decide` for the cap at the first charge that would pass them; `capped_attempt_open()`; and the refusal `numbers.attempt-cap` (`ATTEMPT_CAP`) for an attempt that reaches its cap. `count_uncharged_work(units)` adds a caller's own count to `OperationWork::uncharged`. `Decider::reserve_uncounted_arithmetic` charges as `reserve_exact_arithmetic` does for arithmetic that does not count itself, and takes nothing off the count. `Rational::limbs` is public. New public module `catallax_features::predictor`: `Look`, `Participant`, `Tier`, `Choice`, `Path`, `of_fillet(model, edges, radius)`, `with_log`, and the constants `LOW_COMPLEXITY_CAP` (20,000,000), `HIGH_COMPLEXITY_CAP` (2,000,000), `LOW_COMPLEXITY_SCORE` and `LOOK_READS`. A fillet built through `evaluate_incremental_outcome` runs its exact builders under the cap of its tier before the rolling ball takes over. `catallax_boolean::boolean` refuses `boolean.tolerant-participant` (`TOLERANT_PARTICIPANT`) when either body stores a tolerance on a vertex, an edge or a face, and `Model::tolerant_entity(body)` names the first such entity. `numerical::gap::WORK_CAP` names the checker's refusal `numerical.gap-work-cap`.
Why: roadmap item 3.0B ([Checkpoint 2 decisions, section 4.5](../checkpoints/checkpoint-2-decisions.md#45-item-30b-one-model-and-the-predictor), Q12 and Q13): the predictor's steps 2 to 7, and "tolerant entities never reach the exact intersector". Method, sources of the constants and the measured lines are in [the predictor note](../milestones/3.0B-predictor.md) and [the one-model note](../milestones/3.0B-one-model.md).
Migration: none for a caller of `refund_on_refusal`. An exact fillet attempt that has a fitted construction behind it and reaches its tier's cap now ends there and is built by the rolling ball: no attempt on the frozen corpus reaches one. A caller of `catallax_boolean::boolean` that passes a body with a tolerant entity gets the named refusal before any decision is made. Code that reserves exact arithmetic for comparisons, shifts or operations of a `Real` calls `reserve_uncounted_arithmetic`, so the count is not lowered by what was never counted.
