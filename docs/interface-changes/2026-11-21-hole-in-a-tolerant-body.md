## 2026-11-21: a hole in a body with tolerant entities, and the filtered predicate

Pieces: 3 (topology), 7 (features), the import piece, and the application's build through `evaluate_incremental_outcome`
Change: the refusal `features.numerical-continuation` is removed. A `hole` step whose target is a body with tolerant entities (the result of a fillet the rolling ball built, or of a bend or a twist) is built by the new `catallax_features::hole::through_tolerant::through`, and the outcome is `EvaluationOutcome::Numerical` with the hole's walls named by `Role::Hole`. It builds a through hole of one radius, square to the planar faces it crosses; other cases refuse `hole.numerical-tool`, `hole.numerical-frame`, `hole.numerical-not-clear`, `hole.numerical-oblique-face` or `hole.numerical-crossings`. Every other operation that reads such a body refuses `features.tolerant-input`. New public module `catallax_topology::numerical::predicate`: `Line::point_beyond`, `Line::segment_beyond`, `turn` and `Stages`, the sign of a distance against a reach in three stages (binary64 with an error bound, intervals, rationals). New `numerical::admit_import_under(limits, ...)`, which is `admit_import` with a stated allowance for each edge.
Why: roadmap item 3.0B ([Checkpoint 2 decisions, section 4.5](../checkpoints/checkpoint-2-decisions.md#45-item-30b-one-model-and-the-predictor), Q12): "Any decision with a tolerant participant is 'distance against the sum of tolerances' through a filtered predicate" and "The `features.numerical-continuation` refusal goes away: a hole after a numerical fillet works". See [the one-model note](../milestones/3.0B-one-model.md#a-hole-in-a-body-with-tolerant-entities).
Migration: a caller that matched the code `features.numerical-continuation` matches `features.tolerant-input` for an operation with no construction on a tolerant body, and expects a numerical result where the operation is a through hole. A recipe that ended in a hole after a numerical fillet was refused and now builds or refuses by one of the `hole.numerical-` names.
