## 2026-11-21: relations refuse a field-only envelope

Pieces: application queries (`catallax_app::queries`), measure (`catallax_measure`)
Change: `ApplicationBody::body_relation`, `overlap_volume` and `contact_faces` refuse the new code `app.relation-envelope` when either operand's body is a field-only envelope: the body of a lattice or gyroid step, or of any step that reads one. The new `catallax_app::queries::field_only_envelope(built, step)` returns that refusal for a step of a build, or `None`. Separately, `catallax_measure::solid_gap` and the face-pair distance query give an edge with no line or circle form the proven box of the face that holds it, where it had no box.
Why: roadmap 3.3 step 4. The B-rep carries a field-only step as its region's envelope (PRD section 7). In the step 4 test assembly, a ball in P6's bore at cavity height was stated clear by an exact 0.0464 mm, the gap to the envelope, while the field proved the part's gap between 0.38 and 0.40 mm. Mass already refuses the envelope (`assembly.mass-envelope`), as does STEP export (`field.stand-in-export`). The edge box turns gaps with a lower end of 0, such as turned P3's fillet mitre edges against distant parts, into clear answers with a proven, marked gap.
Migration: a caller that related a part with a lattice or gyroid through `ApplicationBody` now gets `app.relation-envelope`; the field kernel holds that part's material (`catallax_field::assembly::relation`). The model-level `body_relation` is unchanged and still relates whatever bodies it is given; an assembly caller checks `field_only_envelope` for each part first. Distance answers keep their enclosures. One that settled such an edge by the interval search may now set it aside at less work, and a pair that refused `app.relation-gap` or `measure.distance-uncovered` because of such an edge may now answer.
