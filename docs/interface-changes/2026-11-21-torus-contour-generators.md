## 2026-11-21: contour generators of tori, fitted where no exact curve exists

Pieces: application (`catallax_app::views`).
Change: `contour_generators` now answers for ring-torus faces instead of refusing with `app.contour-surface`. Seen along or across the axis the generators are exact circles. Seen obliquely each arc is a fitted cubic B-spline with exact ends, and `ContourArc` gains `fit: Option<ContourFit>` (deviation, rule T4 tolerance, rule T2 target, span count; `None` for exact arcs). An answer with a fitted arc carries one `CONTOUR_FIT_MARK` (`app.contour-fitted`) mark whose bound is the largest tolerance. New refusals: `app.contour-trim` for an oblique torus face bounded by a curve other than its parallels and meridians, `app.contour-fit` when rule T2's target cannot be met in binary64, `app.contour-fit-work` when one fit exceeds its work allowance, and `app.contour-cuts` when two cuts are too close for binary64 angles to separate. Spline faces and horn or spindle torus sheets still refuse with `app.contour-surface`. An arc that runs along existing edges now splits where the bordering face changes, and each piece's `on_faces` comes from the edge spans that hold it, not from the edge's carrier line or circle alone, so `on_faces` holds along the whole arc.
Why: milestone 3.1 needs silhouettes on curved solids, and fillets make tori. The oblique generator of a torus has degree 8, so it is fitted and marked approximate (decision Q14).
Migration: code that builds `ContourArc` values adds `fit: None`. A caller that assumed one arc per carrier along an edge can now see two adjacent arcs with different `on_faces`. Callers that need exact geometry check `fit` per arc or `Answer::is_exact` for the whole answer. Fitted arcs keep their exact ends in `start` and `end`.
