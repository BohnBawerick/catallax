## 2026-11-28: recipe booleans merge sub-tolerance operand planes once

Pieces: 3 (topology), 5 (boolean engine), features (recipe evaluation), 7 (STEP export), verification exchange, SolidDraft
Change: `catallax_boolean::boolean_merging_gaps` is `boolean` for constructing a part, and recipe evaluation now uses it for every boolean step. Before intersecting two different solids, it merges a plane of the tool parallel to a nearby straight-edged target face's plane, when all the tool face corners on it lie strictly within the 1 nm modelling tolerance of that plane. Tilted near-coincident planes are not merged. The tool is copied once with that plane, its corners and straight edges recomputed exactly, and the answer carries one `boolean.sub-tolerance-merge` mark (`SUB_TOLERANCE_MERGE`, kind `Distance`) bounding the copy's largest corner movement. Topology adds `substituted_body`, a copy with listed points, curves and surfaces replaced. `boolean` and `subtract_with_history` are unchanged and still decide every near miss exactly, as does the legacy question exchange that reads recipes of primitives alone.
Why: A sub-nanometre gap between operand planes, such as the 3.5164312091753774e-36 mm gap in the captured SolidDraft bracket, otherwise stayed in the exact model as a sliver face. Every export then had to repair it, and every later operation carried it. Merging it once at construction follows the 1 nm near-coincidence rule in [the architecture](../ARCHITECTURE.md).
Migration: A merged recipe answer is bounded, not exact: its manifest has `exact: false` and the mark, and its result has fewer faces (the captured bracket union has 10 instead of 11). Callers that required `exact: true` or an empty mark log for such a recipe must accept the mark, move the gap above 1 nm, or call `boolean` directly. STEP exports of such results no longer write `step-regularizations.json` for the merged gap. Recipes without a sub-tolerance operand gap give the same answers, statistics and work: the search is charged only when a plane merges. Face origins and edge names are kept.
