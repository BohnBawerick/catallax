# P3 material definition and disputed STEP points

The target remains [`P3-filleted-boss.json`](../../parts/P3-filleted-boss.json), SHA-256 `57e4be2653f8fe2858a6dd95001e530aa1837e80e1d74e5404c0d7e708eae787`. Its scalar edit changes the concave junction radius from 2 to 2.5 mm. It does not change the boss radius, height or the four radius-1 block rounds.

## Independent sections

Write `rho = hypot(x-20, y-15)` and let `r` be the junction radius. The material is the union of these three finite regions:

1. The rounded block, with `0 <= z <= 10` and `d(z) <= x <= 40-d(z)`, `d(z) <= y <= 30-d(z)`. For `z <= 9`, `d(z)=0`; for `9 <= z <= 10`, `d(z)=1-sqrt(1-(z-9)^2)`.
2. The boss, with `rho <= 8` and `10 <= z <= 22`.
3. The junction, with `10 <= z <= 10+r` and `rho <= 8+r-sqrt(r^2-(z-10-r)^2)`.

The radius-1 rounds meet by intersecting their finite side sections. They do not add full cylinders outside the block. The junction's meridian is the inner quarter of the circle centred at `(rho,z)=(8+r,10+r)`. It adds material between the boss and block without extending above `z=10+r`.

Both points reported in the [26 October comparison](2026-10-26-head-to-head-1m.md#correctness-and-historical-comparability) are outside for both radii:

| Point | Material exclusion |
| --- | --- |
| `(40,-10,88/3)` | `y=-10` is outside the block; `z=88/3` exceeds the boss and junction heights. |
| `(80/3,0,22)` | Above the block and junction. Its squared distance from the boss axis is `2425/9`, greater than `8^2`. |

The analytic checker already gives these expected classifications. Changing its expected values to match an imported solid would contradict the recipe.

Useful exact boundary probes follow directly from the sections. At `z=49/5`, each block round has inset `2/5`. At `r=2,z=54/5`, the junction's outer radius is `42/5`. At `r=5/2,z=11`, it is `17/2`. Thus `(28.4,15,11)` changes from outside to inside on the edit, while `(28.5,15,11)` changes from outside to boundary. The boss side and cap remain at `rho=8` and `z=22`.

The independent [exact model regression](../../crates/catallax-features/tests/p3_material.rs) builds the original and edited recipes and checks 69 rational point classifications. It covers both disputed points, their neighbors, all four block rounds, boss side/cap boundaries, the base and the junction's changed material. `cargo test -p catallax-features --test p3_material` passed against source `7a347113d34131a54dc59af2c6f073da2af9099f`. Every query uses the existing 1M work allowance; exact boundary answers must be `ProvenCoincident`, not rounded inside/outside decisions. These are finite semantic probes, not a global set-equality proof.

## Why the two grids disagree

The historical checker uses the bounds of an independently built OpenCASCADE body, including its bounding-box padding. The analytic checker uses exact target bounds. Both generate six coordinates per axis with `k=-1..4` and `lo+(hi-lo)*k/3`. Their 216 points are different. The historical grid avoids the exact supporting-plane alignments at the disputed points; it cannot settle them.

The historical report and current analytic results remain unchanged. A diagnostic pass using their unchanged checkers and the exact historical STEP digests retained these results:

| --- | --- | --- |
| Original | 216 / 0 / 0 | 188 / 28 / 1 |
| Junction radius 2.5 | 216 / 0 / 0 | 188 / 28 / 2 |

## Export versus construction

Independent OpenCASCADE construction from the dimensions classifies both disputed points and their coordinate-wise +/-0.000001 mm neighbors outside. It also gives the junction transition and boundary above.

The standard STEP reader reports valid topology for the historical Catallax files but misclassifies the documented points. At `(40,-10,88/3)`, axis-aligned and oblique line/face queries find no intersections. Moving x or y by 0.000001 mm changes its solid classification to outside; changing only z does not. This is not evidence of real material at that point.

The repository's unhealed `raw_solid` translator exposes a separate file-integrity failure. It translates the STEP solid directly with SameParameter and shape healing disabled. Both variants have seven valid planar faces and six invalid curved faces: the boss cylinder, junction torus and four block-round cylinders. Their cylindrical V domains are unbounded and the torus retains its full periodic chart.

| Variant | Definition volume, mm^3 | Raw imported volume, mm^3 | Raw topology |

| Original | 14428.640126047918 | 17578.90518228015 | Invalid |
| Junction radius 2.5 | 14455.207561827563 | 17547.000718611496 | Invalid |

These observations used `cadquery-ocp==8.0.1.1.0` and the same raw translation path as `tools/step-regressions/test_loopless_faces.py`. Standard-reader healing is not a certificate of the input file's finite trims. A valid raw export and the unchanged disputed-point checks remain required before P3 output acceptance. This note neither changes the writer nor declares P3 complete in SolidDraft.
