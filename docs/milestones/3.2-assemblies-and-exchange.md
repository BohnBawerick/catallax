# Assemblies and exchange

The STEP APIs use the same rigid `Pose` as placed kernel bodies. Each occurrence names a definition and holds a pose from that definition's coordinates to its parent's. A part's model stays in its own coordinates. Two occurrences of a shaft use one product, one B-rep and one set of manufacturing information.

The writer follows OpenCASCADE's ProSTEP Agreement Log item 21 mapping. A `NEXT_ASSEMBLY_USAGE_OCCURRENCE` links the parent and child product definitions. Its context-dependent shape representation links their representations through an `ITEM_DEFINED_TRANSFORMATION` between two frames. Part colours use STEP surface styles. The writer composes the existing part files and relocates their entity references without changing geometry or strings. The reader assigns PMI to its owning product before it resolves datum letters or item names. It refuses cycles, missing placements and PMI without a unique part attachment. [OpenCASCADE STEP guide](https://github.com/Open-Cascade-SAS/OCCT/blob/master/dox/user_guides/step/step.md)

HarmonyWare supplies STEP and IGES translators for SMLib, including a trimmed-surface exchange path. Its public descriptions give no AP242 occurrence or IGES seam encoding to follow. [HarmonyWare STEP products](https://www.harmonyware.com/step_products.html) and [HarmonyWare IGES products](https://www.harmonyware.com/iges_products.html)

The IGES writer uses the type 196 spherical and type 198 toroidal surfaces recognized by OpenCASCADE. A sphere has one circular meridian used twice and two pole uses from its vertex list. A ring torus has two circular seams meeting at one vertex, each with two uses. Both use exact degree endpoints in parameter space. The exporter encloses the rounding of the shared radian curve ranges and adds it to the existing similarity certificate. The outer circle of a torus bounds its distance from its centre for the whole support. [OpenCASCADE IGES parameter conversion](https://github.com/Open-Cascade-SAS/OCCT/blob/V7_8_1/src/IGESToBRep/IGESToBRep_TopoSurface.cxx)

## Reproduce the checks

Use an external directory for artifacts and an environment with `cadquery-ocp==8.0.1.1.0`.

```sh
CATALLAX_ASSEMBLY_FIXTURES=/tmp/assembly-readback \
  cargo test -j 4 -p catallax-import --test assembly_step -- --nocapture
python crates/catallax-import/tests/assembly_readback.py /tmp/assembly-readback

CATALLAX_IGES_FIXTURES=/tmp/iges-readback \
  cargo test -j 4 -p catallax-iges --test contract write_readback_fixtures -- --ignored
python crates/catallax-iges/tests/readback.py /tmp/iges-readback
```

The assembly test writes a drive assembly with one base plate and two occurrences of a shaft in a rotated carrier. It rebuilds the two unique solids, remaps annotations to their rebuilt faces and writes the assembly again. Both files and the 7,974 counted exchange work units matched at one, two and four threads. OpenCASCADE XDE checked both files for names, colours, the shared shaft definition, composed poses, and the faces referenced by two datums, a diameter and two parallelism tolerances. XDE exposes datums used by tolerances. Catallax also reads standalone datums. The IGES check used OpenCASCADE's base B-rep reader and checked the analytic types, seam incidence, validity and volume.

## Coverage

This kernel exchange path covers one rooted STEP product graph, rigid placements, uniform sRGB per part and the existing part PMI types. Geometry follows the existing part writers and readers, including their accepted solid subsets. The assembly report gives local translation and basis rounding alongside the existing part export bounds. Per-occurrence and per-face styles, external STEP references and other assembly encoding styles are outside this path. The IGES writer supports whole spherical and ring toroidal faces. Trimmed spheres and tori remain a follow-on. X3 retains its existing partial coverage because its full bar remains open. X8's overall acceptance also includes the separate P1 C1 to C5 app and drawing checks.
