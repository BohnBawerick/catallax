# 3.3 clash, clearance and mass

Roadmap 3.3 (old M17): "Clash, clear by a stated gap, or unresolved, for every pair." Why here: "Exact overlap already works. Distant and nested pairs are left." Done when: "Every pair in a test assembly gets one of the three answers."

The B-rep query is `catallax_app::queries::body_relation`. It answers `Clash` with a proven positive overlap volume, `Clear` with a proven positive gap, or refuses with a typed reason, which is the unresolved answer. The field's `catallax_field::assembly::relation` stays a test-time yardstick, decision Q16 of the [Checkpoint 2 decisions](../checkpoints/checkpoint-2-decisions.md). [M17-RELATION-MASS.md](../M17-RELATION-MASS.md) has the earlier contract and the mass queries.

The milestone lands in steps, one pull request each:

1. Distant pairs (this note).
2. Nested and overlapping pairs where the Boolean refuses.
3. A certified answer for strata pairs with no closed form that hold the minimum, so their stated gap meets the requested accuracy.
4. The test assembly, with the field relation as yardstick.

Pairs with a tolerant participant, a body carrying a fitted-geometry mark, keep refusing `app.relation-construction-bound` until roadmap 3.0B gives tolerant entities their predicates.

## Step 1: distant pairs

### What refused before

A probe placed copies of P1 to P4 and of a spline loft 1 mm apart, 500 mm apart and turned a quarter turn, and paired block, sphere, cylinder, cone and torus solids at several poses. Of 115 pairs, 64 refused. Three causes covered almost all of them:

- `body_relation` took the minimum over every face pair, each face pair on its own. A face pair far from the minimum still refused `measure.distance-uncovered` when two of its edges had no closed form, for example two circles with non-parallel normals. Every distant or near copy of P1 to P4 refused this way, and the P4 copies spent 43M to 63M work first.
- A block turned by 30 degrees has parallel faces on planes with no rational frame. The planar witness search refused `measure.irrational-plane`.
- Two P3 copies 1 mm apart refused in the overlap Boolean, `intersect.traced-pair-required`, because their torus fillets form a non-coaxial torus pair. The Boolean ran even on solids whose boxes are far apart, at 20M to 43M work for P3 and P4.

### How OpenCASCADE and SMLib do it

OpenCASCADE's `BRepExtrema_DistShapeShape` splits both shapes into vertices, edges and faces, computes a box for each, keeps the pairs whose boxes are nearer than the best distance found, sorts them by box distance and evaluates them in that order with `Extrema`. Its `Extrema_ExtElC::PlanarLineCircleExtrema` answers a line in, or parallel to, a circle's plane by working in that plane; a general line and circle go to a trigonometric equation of degree four, and two circles have a closed form only when they are coplanar. SMLib's `SmApiBrepRelationship` (in `SM_API/src/SmApiQueries.cpp`) first takes the minimum distance between the two boundaries, then classifies one closest point against the other solid to tell separate pairs from containment; step 2 follows it.

Step 1 follows `BRepExtrema_DistShapeShape` for the order and the pruning, and keeps Catallax's exact closed forms for each pair, so a value is exact where the closed form is.

### The change

`catallax_measure::solid_gap` takes the minimum distance between the closed boundaries of two solids that do not cross:

- Each face's strata (the face, its edges, its vertices) and its proven box are computed once. Face pairs are visited in order of their boxes' separation. Once the best distance is no greater than a pair's box separation, that pair and every later one are skipped.
- The strata search of a visited pair skips any strata pair that cannot come below the best distance of the whole search, not only of its own face pair.
- A line parallel to a circle's plane now has a closed form: the stationary pairs of the line's projection into the plane, lifted back along the normal. This is OpenCASCADE's planar line-circle case.
- Two parallel planar faces whose planes have no rational frame no longer refuse. The pairs at the plane gap form a closed region of the overlap of the two projected faces. A boundary point of that region lies on an edge or vertex of one face, where the same minimum is stationary again, and the search visits those strata.
- A strata pair with no closed form whose boxes are nearer than the final minimum is settled face pair by face pair. `catallax_shapes::distance::face_distance_below` runs the existing certified interval search over the two closed faces with the minimum as its ceiling. It discards a cell pair as soon as its covers are that far apart. It either proves the faces no nearer than the minimum or finds a nearer admitted witness. All open face pairs share one cell budget, the certified search's default for one face pair.
- With a witness found, settling only tightens a proven bound, so it runs inside an `OperationAllowance` of as much work again as the closed-form search spent. Planar faces bounded by spline edges make every cell an exact trim test against a spline; without this cap one such pair of the turned loft spent the whole 100M allowance. Without a witness, settling is the only route to an answer and runs within the request's allowance.
- What settling cannot settle widens the stated gap down to the lower bound it proved. The answer then carries the `measure.accuracy-not-reached` mark, the bounded answer kind. The query refuses `measure.distance-uncovered` only without any witness pair.

`body_relation` now:

- proves zero overlap from the two solids' proven boxes when they are strictly apart, without the Boolean;
- calls `solid_gap` for the gap;
- answers `Clear` for any proven positive gap, carrying the mark when the enclosure is wider than the requested accuracy. `app.relation-gap` now means a gap not proven positive.

The [interface note](../interface-changes/2026-11-21-solid-gap.md) lists the API changes.

### Results

Every pair that answered before gives the same enclosure and marks, compared as exact rationals over the 51 answering pairs of the probe and in the existing `assembly_queries` tests. Of the 64 refusals, 60 now answer. 16 of them answer clear with a marked, wider gap, where a strata pair with no closed form lies at the minimum: cone pairs, circles with non-parallel normals and torus pairs turned a quarter turn. ### Tests

```sh
cargo test -p catallax-measure --release --test solid_gap
cargo test -p catallax-app --release --test clash_clearance --test assembly_queries
```

`solid_gap` covers the planar line-circle form, far faces with no closed form, a turned cube's inexact parallel faces and a marked wider gap. `clash_clearance` covers distant, near and turned P2 copies, near P3 copies where the Boolean refuses, the pin in P2's bore, P2 standing in P4's pocket, and identical answers and work at 1, 2 and 4 threads.
