# Field printing controls

The 4.3 exit is the full native-field P6 bracket at 0.01 mm with its cavities
and connected passages preserved. P6 uses a 1 mm enclosing skin, cubic 2 mm
cells, radius-0.3 mm beams and a radius-0.5 mm through bore. Its recipe fixes
the overlap of the infill region and the cavity wall at 0.25 mm.

## Material correspondence

The fine native-field route handles constant axis-aligned cubic lattice
supports with a common period, axis-aligned blocks and vertical cylinders.
It applies when the requested accuracy is smaller than a supported beam radius.
It deforms each squared radial coordinate into its affine chord and keeps the
plan's Boolean expression. Periodic midpoints are knots. Planar constraints
remain exact.

An adaptive octree excludes boxes with one sign. Every remaining terminal
box has a direction with a strictly positive projection against all possibly
active gradients through the entire deformation. The test includes one-sided
gradients at seams and clip creases. Its slope bound dominates the change of
each active carrier over time at the requested displacement. A common flow
then carries the zero set and its material sides to the affine field. This
adapts [Plantinga and Vegter's interval subdivision](https://www.math.rug.nl/~gert/documents/2004/pv-sgp-2004.pdf)
and [Raffalli's separating-direction flow](https://raffalli.eu/pdfs/isotopic.pdf).

The export clips exact planar polygons where an operand can influence the
result and shares all edge subdivisions before triangulating. This follows
the shared-edge principle in [OpenCASCADE](https://dev.opencascade.org/doc/overview/html/occt_user_guides__mesh.html)
and [SMLib](https://github.com/NVIDIA-Omniverse/usd-brep/blob/main/.agents/operations/tessellation.md)
within the native-field route. The writer certifies facet orientation and
separation during binary32 rounding. An interval filter resolves strict
cases and the exact Bernstein test handles the rest. The file certificate
adds the rounding contribution to the field displacement.

## P6 at 0.01 mm

The full P6 recipe exported through the native-field CLI at a recorded
file bound of approximately 0.009997891320 mm. The binary32 displacement
contributed approximately 0.000000752343 mm. The file has 105,843 vertices
and 212,026 facets. The route visited 429,232 proof and clipping cells.

The request used 80,742,140 charged work units, above the unchanged
20,000,000 target and within the 100,000,000 allowance. The work trace
also reports 78,749,667 units of uncharged arithmetic. The saved STL has
SHA-256 `431101d80b1d653250623a24fe399cb953b8950ebd25d0f726988981851552bc`.
The release CLI regression matched the complete certificate, work and STL
bytes at one, two and four threads.

The independent check found one closed outward shell, no self-intersections
and Euler characteristic -170. Exact tests against the saved triangles
found an open line through the bore and a clear path from the bore through
both arms of the cavity. The field certificate covers the complete material
correspondence, including passages outside these independent probes.

## Released baseline

These controls used the released Catallax v0.4.0 CLI with 2,000,000 cells and
1,000,000 vertices. The full-allowance 0.01 mm runs used macOS arm64. The other
runs used Linux x86-64. The field route was explicit in each print request.

| Request | Accuracy | Work limit | Result |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Full P6 | 0.5 mm | 20,000,000 | STL at a recorded 0.5 mm bound. 415,929 work, 146,321 cells, 225,408 vertices and 451,156 facets. |
| Full P6 | 0.01 mm | 100,000,000 | `field.print-budget` at 2,000,001 work when the uniform grid reached the cell cap. No STL. |
| P7 gyroid bracket | 0.01 mm | 100,000,000 | `field.print-classification` at 1,233,317 work. The exact grid-corner classifier could not decide a corner. No STL. |
| P7 gyroid bracket | 0.01 mm | 1,000 | Budget probe reached `numbers.operation-budget`. No STL. |
| Period-4, level-0.25 sheet | 0.01 mm | 20,000,000 | `field.print-detail-topology` at zero work. No STL. |
| Same sheet | 0.125 mm | 20,000,000 | `field.print-detail-topology` at zero work. No STL. |

The P6 coarse result, both full-allowance 0.01 mm refusals and each sheet
refusal match at one, two and four threads, including work and output bytes
where present. The [independent STL checker](../../.claude/skills/verify-catallax/scripts/check-stl.py)
read the coarse P6 file. It found one closed, outward shell, no
self-intersections and Euler characteristic -170. Winding probes matched the
expected material in the skin and beams, the void in each arm, and the void
at four heights through the bore.

The [uniform grid at 0.01 mm](../parked/m19-printing.md#p6-at-recorded-controls)
requires 10,485,760 cells for one layer on a single flat P6 patch. That
baseline motivated the adaptive route above.

## Gyroid scope

[P7](../../parts/P7-gyroid-bracket.json) keeps P6's shell, infill region
and through bore. It uses a level-zero network gyroid with a 2 mm period and
an identity frame. Its composite print route still needs an exact decision at
the unsettled grid corner.
The current CLI reproduced that refusal and work count with no STL.

The [V7c controls](../FIELD.md#terminal-gyroid-output) cover
terminal period-10 network gyroids clipped to rectangular regions. Their
0.1 mm distance bounds and accepted outputs apply to those controls.

The sheet uses the band `|g| <= 0.25` clipped to a 4 × 1 × 1 mm block.
Exact point and box queries support this band. Sheet export requires a
material correspondence certificate for both boundary sheets and their joins
at the clip planes.
