# M1: Catallax 0.1.0 alpha

M1 ships the kernel and verification tooling, not a completed SolidDraft target part. This is the first milestone release. All 0.x releases are alpha GitHub prereleases. crates.io publication, Windows Authenticode and Apple notarisation remain off.

The preparation snapshot is main `ce0c15830db6d1ef693aea4d2c5cf4f68b133041`. Measurements below identify their actual source commits and allowances; they are not relabelled as measurements of the later version-and-note merge. The exact release commit still requires its own green full suite, replay, evidence workflow and non-publishing platform dry run before tagging, as specified in [RELEASING.md](../RELEASING.md).

## 0.1.1 release candidate boundary

The patch candidate is on `release/0.1.1`, cut from main `42179d056be77c590013bbe14883ca980ab46f3c` at the 27 October release freeze. Development continues on main. This is a patch of M1, not completion of M2 or full canonical P1.

The 0.1.0 measurements and parked-work record below remain historical evidence at their named commits. They are not measurements of the 0.1.1 candidate. Publication still requires the final release commit's green branch CI, exact-commit replay and target scoreboard, complete package dry run and the joint SolidDraft acceptance gates in [RELEASING.md](../RELEASING.md). Release fixes change the candidate commit and require affected evidence again.

### Focused results at their stated allowances

- P4 builds and exports valid 15-face STEP at 100M in the offset/shell change, using 11,972,563 work. Its sampled comparison agrees with 266 forward and 421 reverse samples. Construction is 2,705.572 ms and native field display 41,902.492 ms on the measured shared host. It still refuses at the 1M scoreboard setting and misses the interaction target.
- P1's full eight-cut recipe still refuses at `slot-cut-4` at 100M. The latest qualified prefix through cut 2 drops from 34,721,547 to 29,208,299 work; paired median wall time drops from 3,473.195 to 2,948.698 ms. That prefix still exceeds the 1 s target. Its sampled comparison remains unresolved after display exhausts the unchanged boundary-sample allowance. This is not full-P1 acceptance.
- Eight of ten focused conical recipes change from coverage refusals to answers, with eight sampled comparisons agreeing. The two unsupported domains remain explicit refusals. This does not make P0's general non-coaxial cone or torus branch tracing complete.
- P6's explicit 0.5 mm print request produces 451,156 facets and 225,408 vertices with independent watertightness, orientation and cavity checks. The strict mass stand-in imports as two solids and one massless envelope shell. Maximum inertia interval width is 9.4757444002e-10 mm5 against the unchanged 1e-9 mm5 request. Its 392,552.333 ms strict export and 153,364.644 ms print remain slow. These explicit 100M queries are distinct from the ordinary 1M scoreboard adapters.

### Regressions and remaining limits

Application resource guards deliberately turn some earlier long-running successful edits into `application.work-limit` refusals. In the resource-bound change's measured scenarios, hole-origin x values 1e-12, 1e-3 and zero had answered before and refused afterward. Tiny inputs that previously aborted or timed out now refuse without publishing partial geometry. The legacy verification replay remains unchanged because it retains its own allowance. This is a responsiveness/safety trade-off, not a repaired geometry capability.

P0 general branches, full P1, P4 at the scoreboard allowance, field display coverage and the SolidDraft application loop remain open. No budget, sample allowance or geometric tolerance was loosened to pass M1. Sampled agreement and successful STEP import do not supply a global boundary certificate or the unmeasured exported-volume check.

## M2 must-show list

PLAN section 10.1 requires all of the following for 0.2.0:

1. Complete P1 builds and edits in SolidDraft on Catallax inside the interactive targets, with the other kernel removed. Prefix success and a standalone kernel build do not close this gate.
2. Coplanar booleans are sound in both kernels. Keep the already-landed set rules and close the complete application/target evidence.
3. The application verification loop runs on GitHub's runners.
4. Both prototypes have decisions. The current verdicts are park, not promotion; any replacement or resumed experiment must retain its written proof and decision criteria.
5. The first tolerance-core piece is merged with its actual consumer proof. Landed evidence types alone are not acceptance; retain the exact/bounded distinction, whole-face bound and unchanged-answer/worker checks.
