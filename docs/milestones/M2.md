# M2: application loop and first tolerance-core consumer

M2 is not reached. SolidDraft runs on Catallax, the application loop and the first tolerance-core consumer have landed, but complete canonical P1 preview/display and background proof have not met the roadmap's gates.

The [kernel allowance profile](../M2-P1-WORK-PROFILE.md) measures
canonical P1 at 41,647,298 units for the eager application build. It still refuses
at the unchanged 20M allowance with `application.work-limit` during `slot-cut-6`.
That follow-up records the separate construction-route check and its scope;
it supplies no SolidDraft acceptance or timing pass.

This is a status review on 4 November 2026 against Catallax main `98f3fe03d0cbb80ae75e84a57e89bc7df04d63eb`. Linked SolidDraft changes identify their own kernel pins. Historical measurements below are not measurements of this snapshot. This note is not a release announcement or a record of sponsor delivery.

The [26 October 1M scoreboard](2026-10-26-head-to-head-1m.md), merged in [the scoreboard refresh](https://github.com/BohnBawerick/catallax/pull/141), records five of thirteen original/edit constructions, five original displays and four original/edit display pairs. P0 contributes three answering construction pairs out of eight. P1 refuses at `slot-cut-1`; its original/edit requests consume 999,999/999,997 work. These are historical kernel CLI results, not current application acceptance.

The later [bracket width-edit application evidence](https://github.com/ZIGRAWBOHNBAWERICK/soliddraft/pull/71) includes checked STEP and surviving history, but explicitly does not claim canonical P1. Its hosted edit took 2381.860415 ms against the unchanged 1000 ms budget. Acceptance under a known timing-gap policy is not a strict latency pass.

## Parked work and remaining exit

The prototype park decisions remain historical decisions, not proof that later production fillets cannot work. Resume or replace a prototype only under its written construction, whole-face proof, edit-identity and preservation gates.

To reach M2, publish complete canonical P1 construction and parameter-edit evidence through SolidDraft, with selectable preview/display inside the applicable 100/500/1000 ms target and proof inside 2000 ms under the unchanged allowance. Include the coplanar/fast-route checks, names, export read-back and pending/failed-proof behaviour required by [PLAN section 9](../PLAN.md#9-target-parts). Scoped feature successes cannot substitute for that run.

## What M3 must show

[M3](M3.md) requires the P0 near-miss class, application holes/end conditions/elementary fillets/chamfers, complete P2 and P3 preview/proof acceptance, and proven STEP and mesh exports. The first tolerance-core consumer does not establish general tolerance-policy composition or near-miss-class completion.
