M22's must-show list is met by [the merged closed-mesh import](https://github.com/BohnBawerick/catallax/pull/369). Exact classification and polyhedron-volume agreement are demonstrated directly; the generic comparison harness still cannot pair unnamed field mesh faces.

## Historical size observations

[The merged PR](https://github.com/BohnBawerick/catallax/pull/369) reports a release-build, one-worker binary-STL sphere with 199,808 triangles. The field compiled in 0.41 s and computed exact moments in 0.47 s; B-rep construction took 5.39 s and exact volume 1.72 s. Reported peak memory was 2.7 GB, dominated by the B-rep; the earlier ledger records the field below 0.1 GB. Compilation/build work was 5.7M/14.5M units respectively, within the unchanged 20M application allowance. These are landing observations, not current performance qualification or an end-to-end timing promise.

The PR also reports a 998,000-triangle field-only experiment. It does not raise or bypass the production limit for milestone acceptance. A million unmerged triangles would make B-rep storage expensive; larger supported imports remain later work.

## Remaining coverage

The [committed results and limits](../interface-changes/2026-11-11-closed-mesh-import.md#results) state that the field registers no named mesh face. Consequently the comparison harness reports `import_mesh` not comparable, a ray from a field boundary point remains `Indeterminate`, and field mass of a Boolean containing a mesh refuses with `field.print-coverage`. The exact-volume tests above supply the required two-kernel agreement without claiming generic named-face comparison.

The reader accepts closed solids, including cavities and separate lumps; it does not reconstruct a clean editable part from an open scan. Reference scans and clean reconstruction belong to M23. No completion of M16's named-face services, mesh-Boolean mass coverage, M23 or M26 follows from M22.
