## P0 and target-part evidence

The [26 October 1M report](2026-10-26-head-to-head-1m.md) records near-miss construction work of 4,202/4,167 for original/edit, display work of 453,453 each, and passing historical STEP/name checks. P2 constructs and exports, but its edited display exhausts 1M. P3 constructs and displays at both radii. Those kernel CLI results predate the later application and export fixes.

The canonical P3 application fixture recorded a 1317.802 ms junction edit and 2107.888 ms reopen against unchanged 1000 ms gates. It did not request manufacturing export. The [P3 material and disputed-file note](2026-10-27-p3-material.md) preserves the earlier invalid raw STEP and classification observations. Later repairs must be judged on their own exported files; neither old failures nor focused new passes are a fresh complete M3 qualification.

## Parked work and remaining exit

The hard-intersection candidates remain parked as documented in [M2](M2.md). No change in this review promotes them or waives section 12 item 11. Import [local-tolerance evidence](https://github.com/BohnBawerick/catallax/pull/266) concerns repaired source faces, not general modelling near misses.

To reach M3, qualify the P0 near-miss class and tolerance-core remainder, then publish complete canonical P2/P3 application runs with separate preview/display and background-proof timing, unchanged deterministic allowances, parameter-edit name survival and checked STEP/mesh outputs. Keep every raw failure and timing miss visible. A ready feature, a green advisory scenario or one answering near-miss fixture is not the milestone.
