## R12 contact diagnostics

The [fillet inventory](../../parts/fillets/cases.jsonl) adds five R12 controls. These preserve the existing operation definitions. A contact upper bound does not prove that every smaller radius builds, or that equality builds. Existing refusals report that distinction and say `maximum unknown` when a fitting maximum is unproved.

| Case | Required result |
| --- | --- |
| `r12-cube-at-contact` | A 10 mm cube with one radius-10 strip builds exactly. |
| `r12-cube-past-contact` | Radius 10.001 keeps the terminal radius-10 roll-over result and retains the authored request. |
| `r12-legacy-plane-bound` | The version-3 refusal names the departed face and the exact 10 mm contact bound. |
| `r12-rim-rational-bound` | A refused rim reports the exact 1/3 mm contact bound separately from its 0.333 mm legacy hint. |
| `r12-generator-bound` | A plane/cylinder generator reports its exact 4 mm strict offset-contact bound and source supports. |

The existing `partial-oblique` case checks an unknown maximum. [Distance regressions](../../crates/catallax-features/tests/refusal_limits.rs) keep a consumed shell cavity successful and an uncovered shell's maximum unknown. [Sharp thicken regressions](../../crates/catallax-features/tests/sharp_thicken.rs) check the bound where a circular projection sector reaches its axis. A smallest-gap proof alone cannot turn cavity consumption into a shell failure under section 4.7 of RECIPE.md. Such a product change would need a separate recipe decision.
