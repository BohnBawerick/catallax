## Remaining coverage

The [fixed-sweep contract](../interface-changes/2026-11-11-fixed-spline-sweeps.md) requires positive weights and strictly ordered normal projections of extracted Bézier controls. This proves that whole polygon sections occupy distinct planes. Returning, self-crossing or tangent sections still refuse with `sweep.spline-admissibility-unproven`; curved profiles, spline chains and rotating frames retain coverage refusals. The field compiler still refuses spline paths. None of these refusals is reclassified as impossible geometry.

[LOFT-CASES](../LOFT-CASES.md#admission-and-remaining-coverage) retains the nonparallel/nonaffine, singular-section and unproved embedding/separation limits. SolidDraft's Loft command/document conversion, wider sweep laws, later spline cuts and independent field coverage remain their own work. A passed M7 demonstration does not close M10, M18 or M26.
