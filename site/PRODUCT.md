## Product Purpose

This is one static site for Catallax and SolidDraft. It states the intended direction of both projects, shows evidence for the kernel, and asks visitors to read the code, fund the work or get in touch.

Catallax is an open-source geometry kernel under the MIT licence. It is the kernel for SolidDraft and for anyone else who wants it. It serves the mechanical industry: machined parts, printed parts, drawings, STEP exchange with other CAD tools, FEA and large assemblies.

Success means a visitor can verify a claim, reach the code, sponsor the work or send an email, on any device and with any input method. [inferred]

## Positioning

One recipe, the feature tree, is the master. An exact B-rep kernel and a signed distance field kernel evaluate the same recipe definitions. Each operation has one mathematical definition shared by both kernels.

The argument for free software is the page's own: code is not scarce, so a public kernel lets a solo engineer or a new CAD product enter the market without buying permission from an incumbent.

## Operating Context

- Visitors reach the page from GitHub, shared links and word of mouth. [inferred]
- They often check claims against linked material: `evidence.json`, `comparison-results.json`, the command transcript and the repository.
- The site is static, served by GitHub Pages from `site/` on pushes to `main`. No package install or build step is needed to serve it.
- `comparison.html` and the homepage benchmark table are generated by `render/comparison.py`. The stat counters are written by `render/collect-evidence.mjs`.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Keep the owner's product claims as written, including SolidDraft running natively on Catallax. Do not edit the copy per release, and do not require current-backend, "Not yet" or "in design" distinctions.
- Never claim the paid apps fund the work.
- Keep the special-thanks section near the bottom, with the Fornjot non-affiliation statement and links only to work the repository actually cites.
- Never hand-edit generated numbers. `render/comparison.py --check` rejects a stale page or homepage summary.
- Counters and robustness figures are historical snapshots, not live measurements of `main`.
- The hero, anatomy diagrams and assembly film are illustrations. They are not kernel output, and the page must not present them as such.

Open decisions:

- `evidence.json` records the bracket drawing as coming from the SMLib backend and "not evidence of Catallax integration". The bare-bones page labels it as SolidDraft CLI output and does not claim that this drawing proves Catallax integration. The provenance file remains in the published directory but is not linked beside the drawing.
