Checkpoints 3 and 4. Where the kernel stands.

Phases 3 and 4 of the roadmap end with one measurement. We built the same parts in Catallax, OpenCASCADE and SMLib, imported 3,000 public STEP files, and scored 61 coverage lines. Catallax 0.5.0 is the release these numbers measure. SolidDraft 0.8.0 pins the same kernel.

Edit-chain scoreboard

Each target part is built, then built again after one dimension changes. The test parts are P0, eight hard intersection cases, and P1 to P5: a bracket, a collar, a filleted boss, a shelled tray and a flanged housing. Three phase-2 targets follow: a threaded boss with a spring, a lofted duct and a curved housing. The real parts are ten files from the STEP census below. On each real part, every kernel drills a hole, then changes its radius.

One definition of done for all three kernels: both versions build, both STEP exports open in OpenCASCADE as valid closed solids with the producer's face count, and no independent check finds a wrong value. On a real part, the drilled hole and the radius edit must each reduce the independently measured volume by more than one millionth of the source volume. The filleted boss's STEP files must also read as valid with OpenCASCADE's healing turned off.

Where Catallax falls short

  • The general cone and general torus test parts are refused. Catallax cannot yet trace where those surfaces cross.
  • All three phase-2 targets are refused. The threaded boss stops at the threaded union, the lofted duct at the outer loft, and the curved housing at the spline cut before its fillet.
  • On one real part, 00000173, the drilled hole is refused.
  • The flanged housing counts as done, but the separate name check finds 16 unnamed entities.

OpenCASCADE fails the flanged housing's build. SMLib fails it too, along with three of the hard intersection cases and the collar's checked values. On two real parts, SMLib's hole adds volume.

3,000 real STEP files

The census takes the same 3,000 models from the public ABC collection as Checkpoint 2, with the same frozen source hashes and selection. Opening is refused on 40 of them. The headline counts complete-source, occurrence-preserving exports that a separate OpenCASCADE reader validates as solids. Full solid admission and at least one observed valid exported solid are separate measures.

Import is still the largest gap between Catallax and OpenCASCADE. The two figures are not measured at one tolerance: OpenCASCADE repairs each file with its default healing before the check. The 767 counts Catallax's complete-source valid exports and the 2,701 counts OpenCASCADE's healed-valid files, so they use different protocols. A direct comparison still needs a common protocol and a target.

Against the previous release, 0.4.0, eight more files export as valid solids and five more open as solids. No file that exported validly at the frozen baseline was lost. At the frozen baseline, one solid in file 00002204 opened. That file now runs out of its work allowance while opening.

Coverage of the reference kernels

The coverage list has 61 lines, each a capability that OpenCASCADE or SMLib has. A line scores 1 when it meets its written bar, 0.5 for working evidence in a limited domain, and 0 for none.

Every line has working evidence. One line, spline editing, meets its full bar. This recount uses the adopted acceptance bars. The older 55% and 61% figures used their historical scoring, so the lower share alone does not show a lost capability.

What phases 3 and 4 delivered

  • A tolerance-first core: an edge gap is admitted at a measured local tolerance or refused, and exact and tolerant geometry live in one model.
  • Manufacturing information: the P1 bracket's PMI travels in AP242 STEP, and OpenCASCADE reads 13 of its 14 items. SolidDraft drawings use the kernel's section, silhouette and hidden-line curves.
  • Assemblies: STEP assemblies round-trip shared parts, placements, names and colours. On a 78-pair test assembly, the clash and clearance check gives 38 clear, two clash and 38 named unresolved answers.
  • First builds of bend, twist, curvature-continuous fillets, mesh Booleans and mesh decimation.
  • The field builds the flanged housing's fillet on its own. The recorded comparison checked 3,192 vertices within 9.2e-8 mm.
  • Printing: P6 writes a closed STL with a bound of 0.009997891320 mm.
  • Scans: a measured sphere scan becomes an analytic sphere, with a largest point residual of 0.051628 mm against a requested 0.06 mm.
  • In SolidDraft 0.8.0, all 131 application scenarios passed their checks on the Mac, and 118 met every timing budget.
