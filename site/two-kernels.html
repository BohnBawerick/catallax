One recipe. Two kernels.

The feature tree is the master. Each operation has one written definition, and two independent kernels evaluate it. Neither converts the other's result after each step.

How a part is checked

  1. Recipe

    The feature tree: blocks, sketches, extrusions, Booleans, fillets. Faces and edges are named by the operation that made them.

  2. B-rep kernel

    Exact faces and edges, for STEP export and measurement.

    Field kernel

    For any point: inside, outside or on the surface, with a proven bound on the distance.

  3. Solve-time limit T · TBD

    A kernel that runs past T on a case leaves it unsolved.

  4. Compare, both ways

    Forward: every display-mesh vertex of the B-rep is checked against the field. Reverse: points on the field's boundary are projected onto the exact B-rep faces.

  5. Verdict

    AgreeDisagreeUnresolved

    Missing proof stays unresolved. It never becomes agreement.

Agreement is a sampled check, not a certificate over the whole part. Two kernels can agree on the same wrong shape, so each is also checked against the shared definition.
